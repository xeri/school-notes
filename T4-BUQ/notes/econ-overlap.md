# Business vs Economics: shared topics, different answers

Many Business topics are also in the Economics course, but the two exams want different answers. **Business asks what a change means for one firm and what it should do. Economics asks what it means for the market or the whole economy.** Write the Business version in this exam.

Sources: Business notes in this folder; Economics notes in the T4-ECQ folder (`firms-labour.md`, `foundations.md`, `markets.md`, `elasticity-failure.md`, `macroeconomics.md`, `trade.md`). Section names are given so you can check each row.

## Three habits to switch off for this paper

1. **Diagrams.** Business diagrams are the break-even chart, the business cycle, the inventory control chart, the product life cycle, the average-cost curve for economies of scale, organisation charts and motivation diagrams. No demand and supply or foreign-exchange diagrams.
2. **Ending at the economy.** A Business chain ends at the firm's sales, costs, profit or cash, and its decision — not at GDP or inflation.
3. **The firm in the case is always the subject.** "Consumers" and "the economy" earn little; "CCC's Sunday customers" and "the \$1320 cash balance" earn application marks.
