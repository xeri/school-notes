Pre-Q Basic	Marketing?	Anticipating, understanding and satisfying customer needs at a profit	
Pre-Q Basic	Market?	Wherever buyers and sellers are willing and able to trade	Not a physical place
Pre-Q Basic	Market orientation?	Basing products on market research into customer wants	
Pre-Q Basic	Product orientation?	Focusing on products and processes without market research	
Pre-Q Basic	Market segmentation?	Grouping consumers with similar characteristics and buying habits	
Pre-Q Basic	Target market?	Consumers a firm designs its products and marketing for	
Pre-Q Basic	Niche market?	Small, specific part of a larger market	
Pre-Q Basic	Mass market?	Large market with broad appeal	
Pre-Q Basic	Market share?	One firm's sales as a % of total market sales	
Pre-Q Cloze	Market share = ({{c1::firm's sales}} ÷ {{c2::total market sales}}) × 100	Use the same period and basis	
Pre-Q Cloze	Market growth = ({{c1::change in market size}} ÷ {{c2::original market size}}) × 100		A percentage-change formula
Pre-Q Basic	Price vs non-price competition?	Cutting prices vs competing on features, image or service	
Pre-Q Basic	Primary (field) research?	Collecting new data for the present purpose	
Pre-Q Basic	Secondary (desk) research?	Using data that already exists	
Pre-Q Basic	Qualitative data?	Opinions and reasons: explains why	
Pre-Q Basic	Quantitative data?	Numerical data that can be measured and compared	
Pre-Q Basic	Sample (research)?	Group selected to represent the whole population	
Pre-Q Basic	Random vs quota sample?	Everyone has an equal chance vs set numbers per segment	
Pre-Q Basic	Bias (research)?	Flaw in method making results unrepresentative	
Pre-Q Cloze	Research bias types: {{c1::sampling}}, {{c2::questionnaire}}, {{c3::response}}		3 types
Pre-Q Cloze	Marketing mix (4Ps): {{c1::product}}, {{c2::price}}, {{c3::promotion}}, {{c4::place}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Core}} benefits: what the product does		Core / tangible / augmented
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Tangible}} attributes: what the product is made of and looks like		Core / tangible / augmented
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Augmented}} benefits: extras such as the brand or after-sales service		Core / tangible / augmented
Pre-Q Basic	Invention vs innovation?	Discovering something new vs turning it into a saleable product	
Pre-Q Basic	Unique selling point (USP)?	Feature or benefit that differentiates a product from rivals	
Pre-Q Basic	Product differentiation?	Making a product distinguishable from rivals'	
Pre-Q Basic	Branding?	Creating distinctive, lasting perceptions of a product in consumers' minds	
Pre-Q Basic	Brand image?	How customers perceive the brand	
Pre-Q Cloze	Product life cycle: {{c1::introduction}}, {{c2::growth}}, {{c3::maturity}}, {{c4::decline}}	Development stage sometimes shown first	4 stages
Pre-Q Basic	Extension strategy?	Action at maturity or decline to prolong sales	
Pre-Q Basic	Product portfolio: why have one?	Products in growth balance those in decline, spreading risk	
Pre-Q Basic	Cost-plus pricing?	Adding a mark-up to unit cost	
Pre-Q Basic	Competitor pricing?	Basing the price on rivals' prices	
Pre-Q Basic	Penetration pricing?	Low launch price to build sales and market share	
Pre-Q Basic	Price skimming?	High launch price, lowered later	
Pre-Q Basic	Premium pricing?	High price to signal high quality	
Pre-Q Basic	Psychological pricing?	Price set just below a round number	
Pre-Q Basic	Loss leader?	Price below cost to draw customers to other items	Not the same as penetration
Pre-Q Basic	Contribution pricing?	Price set from variable cost, contributing to fixed costs	
Pre-Q Basic	Price discrimination?	Different prices for different customer groups	Based on PED and incomes
Pre-Q Basic	Price elasticity of demand (PED)?	Responsiveness of quantity demanded to a price change	
Pre-Q Cloze	PED = {{c1::% change in quantity demanded}} ÷ {{c2::% change in price}}	Ignore the minus sign	Quantity on top
Pre-Q Basic	PED value for inelastic demand?	Between zero and one, ignoring the sign	
Pre-Q Basic	PED value for elastic demand?	Greater than one	
Pre-Q Cloze	Inelastic demand: a price rise {{c1::increases}} total revenue		
Pre-Q Cloze	Elastic demand: a price rise {{c1::reduces}} total revenue		
Pre-Q Cloze	Demand is likely inelastic for {{c1::essentials}}, products with {{c2::few substitutes}} and {{c3::strong brands}}		3 signs
Pre-Q Cloze	Sales needed to match old revenue after a price cut = {{c1::old revenue}} ÷ {{c2::new price}}	Round up to a whole unit	
Pre-Q Basic	Why does matching old revenue not match old profit?	Extra units add variable costs	Think contribution
Pre-Q Basic	Promotion?	Communicating with customers to inform and persuade	Not just advertising
Pre-Q Basic	Above-the-line vs below-the-line promotion?	Mass media vs methods without mass media	
Pre-Q Basic	Informative vs persuasive advertising?	Gives facts vs urges buying over rivals	
Pre-Q Basic	Sales promotion?	Short-term incentive to boost sales, e.g. coupons	
Pre-Q Basic	How to judge a promotion's cost-effectiveness?	Extra contribution vs campaign cost	
Pre-Q Basic	Social media promotion: benefit vs limitation?	Cheap two-way reach; complaints are public	
Pre-Q Basic	Distribution channel?	Route from producer to consumer, including intermediaries	
Pre-Q Basic	Intermediary?	Middleman between producer and consumer	
Pre-Q Basic	Wholesaler?	Buys in bulk from producers and sells small amounts to retailers	
Pre-Q Cloze	Distribution channels: {{c1::direct}}, {{c2::retailer}}, {{c3::wholesaler}}, {{c4::agent}}		4 channels
Pre-Q Basic	E-commerce?	Buying and selling goods and services online	
Pre-Q Basic	E-commerce for business: opportunity vs threat?	Global reach; instant price comparison by customers	
Pre-Q Basic	E-commerce for consumers: benefit vs risk?	Wider choice; can't see or try the product	
Pre-Q Basic	Marketing strategy?	Plan of marketing objectives and the actions to reach them	
Pre-Q Basic	Coherent marketing mix?	All 4Ps support each other and the target market	
Pre-Q Basic	SWOT analysis?	Internal strengths and weaknesses; external opportunities and threats	
Pre-Q Cloze	Role of marketing: {{c1::identify needs}}, {{c2::satisfy needs}}, {{c3::keep loyalty}}, {{c4::anticipate change}}		4 roles
Pre-Q Basic	Why build customer relationships?	Keeping customers is cheaper than winning new ones	
Pre-Q Cloze	Why spending patterns change: {{c1::income}}, {{c2::tastes}}, {{c3::age structure}}, {{c4::technology}}, {{c5::substitutes}}		5 causes
Pre-Q Cloze	Why markets are more competitive: {{c1::globalisation}}, {{c2::online price comparison}}, {{c3::cheaper entry through technology}}		3 reasons
Pre-Q Basic	Niche market: main risk?	Vulnerable to a single change in taste	
Pre-Q Basic	Primary research: advantage vs drawback?	Specific and up to date; slow and expensive	
Pre-Q Basic	Secondary research: advantage vs drawback?	Cheap and quick; may be out of date	
Pre-Q Cloze	Primary research methods: {{c1::questionnaires}}, {{c2::interviews}}, {{c3::focus groups}}, {{c4::observation}}		4 methods
Pre-Q Cloze	Secondary sources: {{c1::sales records}}, {{c2::government statistics}}, {{c3::trade journals}}, {{c4::commercial reports}}		4 sources
Pre-Q Basic	Online survey: limitation?	Reaches only people online; no follow-up questions	
Pre-Q Basic	Focus group: benefit vs limitation?	Detailed opinions; small sample and costly	
Pre-Q Basic	Pie chart vs line graph?	Shares of a whole vs a trend over time	
Pre-Q Cloze	Judging research reliability: {{c1::sample selection}}, {{c2::sample size}}, {{c3::question wording}}, {{c4::date}}, {{c5::who paid}}		5 checks
Pre-Q Cloze	Packaging functions: {{c1::protect}}, {{c2::inform}}, {{c3::promote the brand}}, {{c4::convenience}}		4 functions
Pre-Q Basic	New products: benefit vs limitation?	Fill a market gap; costly, and many fail	
Pre-Q Basic	Maturity stage: marketing?	Defend share with competitive prices and reminder ads	
Pre-Q Cloze	Extension strategies: {{c1::more advertising}}, {{c2::price cut}}, {{c3::new features}}, {{c4::new markets}}, {{c5::new packaging}}		5 strategies
Pre-Q Basic	Price skimming suits?	New, unique or high-tech products	
Pre-Q Cloze	Consumer protection: goods must be {{c1::fit for purpose}}, {{c2::safe}} and {{c3::as described}}		3 requirements
Pre-Q Cloze	Why enter foreign markets: {{c1::saturated home market}}, {{c2::more customers}}, {{c3::spread risk}}, {{c4::extend life cycle}}		4 reasons

Pre-Q Basic	Product-oriented business?	Focuses on its products and processes, without market research	Opposite of market-oriented
Pre-Q Basic	Market trend?	General direction of change in sales over time	
Pre-Q Basic	Sampling bias?	Some people less likely to be included in the sample	Who is asked
Pre-Q Basic	Questionnaire bias?	Misleading or badly worded questions	What is asked
Pre-Q Basic	Response bias?	People answer untruthfully, e.g. to seem socially acceptable	How people answer
Pre-Q Basic	Brand loyalty?	Customers repeatedly buying the same brand	Allows a premium price
Pre-Q Basic	Personal selling: best used for?	Explaining a complex or expensive product	
Pre-Q Basic	Public relations / sponsorship: purpose?	Build image and goodwill	
Pre-Q Basic	Direct mail and email promotion: advantage?	Targets known customers cheaply	
Pre-Q Basic	Marketing budget?	Amount a firm can spend on marketing; limits promotion choice	
Pre-Q Basic	Channel of distribution?	Every person or firm a product passes through from producer to consumer	
Pre-Q Basic	Multi-channel distribution?	Using several channels of distribution at once
