Pre-Q Basic	Start-up capital?	Finance needed to begin trading	
Pre-Q Basic	Capital vs revenue expenditure?	Spending on non-current assets vs running costs	
Pre-Q Basic	Working capital?	Finance available for day-to-day operations	Not the bank balance
Pre-Q Cloze	Working capital = {{c1::current assets}} − {{c2::current liabilities}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Retained profit?	Profit kept in the business rather than distributed	
Pre-Q Basic	Overdraft?	Short-term facility to go into deficit on a bank account	
Pre-Q Basic	Trade credit?	Buying from a supplier now and paying later	
Pre-Q Basic	Debenture?	Long-term loan to a company, usually at fixed interest	
Pre-Q Basic	Hire purchase?	Instalments plus interest; owned once fully paid	Not the same as leasing
Pre-Q Basic	Leasing?	Paying to use an asset without buying it	
Pre-Q Basic	Sale and leaseback?	Selling a non-current asset and renting it back	
Pre-Q Basic	Venture capital?	Investment in return for a share of ownership and management	Think business angels
Pre-Q Basic	Microfinance?	Very small loans to people banks won't lend to	
Pre-Q Basic	Crowdfunding?	Raising small amounts from many people, usually online	
Pre-Q Basic	Collateral?	An asset offered as security for a loan	
Pre-Q Basic	Gearing?	Proportion of a firm's finance that is borrowed	
Pre-Q Cloze	Gearing = ({{c1::non-current liabilities}} ÷ {{c2::capital employed}}) × 100	Highly geared above about 60%	
Pre-Q Basic	Debt vs equity finance?	Debt is repaid with interest; equity is never repaid	
Pre-Q Basic	Revenue?	Income from sales: price × quantity	
Pre-Q Cloze	Cost of sales = {{c1::opening inventory}} + {{c2::purchases}} − {{c3::closing inventory}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Profit for the year?	Profit after interest and tax	
Pre-Q Basic	Non-current asset?	Asset kept for more than a year	e.g. premises
Pre-Q Basic	Current asset?	Asset expected to become cash within a year	e.g. inventory
Pre-Q Basic	Current liability?	Debt due within a year	e.g. overdraft
Pre-Q Basic	Intangible asset?	Non-current asset that cannot be seen, e.g. a patent	
Pre-Q Basic	Equity?	Share capital plus retained earnings	Total assets minus total liabilities
Pre-Q Basic	Liquidity?	Ability to meet short-term debts as they fall due	
Pre-Q Basic	Insolvency?	Being unable to pay debts when they are due	
Pre-Q Basic	Overtrading?	Expanding faster than working capital allows	
Pre-Q Basic	Profitability?	How well a firm turns sales and capital into profit	
Pre-Q Basic	Cash-flow forecast?	Estimate of future cash inflows and outflows	
Pre-Q Basic	Income statement?	Summary of revenue, costs and profit over a period	
Pre-Q Cloze	Income statement sections: {{c1::trading account}}, {{c2::profit and loss account}}, {{c3::appropriation account}}		3 accounts
Pre-Q Basic	Statement of financial position?	What the business owns and owes on one date	
Pre-Q Basic	Why match the term of finance to its purpose?	Long-term assets need long-term finance	Why not buy a building with an overdraft?

Pre-Q Basic	Retained profit: limitation?	New or small firms may have little	
Pre-Q Basic	Overdraft: key danger?	Repayable on demand	
Pre-Q Basic	Bank loan: advantage vs drawback?	Fixed sum and term; interest and security needed	
Pre-Q Basic	Trade credit: risk of paying late?	Lost discounts and future supplies refused	
Pre-Q Basic	Share issue: advantage vs drawback?	Permanent capital; dilutes control	
Pre-Q Basic	Government grant: drawback?	Conditions attached, such as keeping jobs

Pre-Q Basic	Why is cash vital?	Bills must be paid on time or the firm fails	
Pre-Q Basic	Profit vs cash flow?	Revenue minus expenses vs money actually moving	
Pre-Q Basic	Why can a credit sale give profit but no cash?	Revenue is recorded now; cash arrives later	
Pre-Q Basic	Is a loan revenue?	No: cash comes in, but it is not revenue	
Pre-Q Cloze	Net cash flow = {{c1::total receipts}} − {{c2::total payments}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Closing balance = {{c1::opening balance}} + {{c2::net cash flow}}	Closing balance becomes next month's opening	
Pre-Q Basic	How to find a missing payment in a forecast?	Total payments minus the other payments	Work backwards
Pre-Q Basic	Negative net cash flow but positive closing balance?	Still solvent; no overdraft needed yet	
Pre-Q Basic	Negative closing balance in a forecast?	Overdraft or other finance is needed
