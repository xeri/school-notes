#deck:Business::5 Financial information and decisions
Definition of start-up capital	Finance needed to begin trading — premises, equipment, initial stock
Definition of working capital	Current assets minus current liabilities
Is working capital the bank balance?	No — it includes inventory and receivables, less short-term debts
Why match the term of finance to the purpose?	An overdraft can be recalled long before a building pays for itself
Three internal sources of finance	Retained profit, sale of unwanted assets, owner's savings
Retained profit: advantage and limit	No interest and no loss of control; limited by past profits
Bank loan: advantage and drawback	A fixed sum with planned repayments; interest, and security usually required
Overdraft: advantage and the key danger	Flexible short-term cover with interest only on what is used; it is repayable on demand
Share capital: advantage and drawback	No repayment date or interest; dilutes ownership and control
Definition of a debenture	A long-term loan to a company, usually at a fixed rate of interest
Trade credit: advantage and drawback	Effectively free delay in paying suppliers; lost discounts and strained relations if abused
Difference between leasing and hire purchase	Leasing never gives ownership; hire purchase ends in ownership
Definition of crowdfunding	Raising small amounts from many people online, which also tests demand
Definition of microfinance	Small loans to very small businesses without conventional collateral
Seven criteria for choosing a source of finance	Amount, purpose, duration, cost, effect on control, risk, legal form of the business
Difference between profit and cash flow	Profit is revenue minus expenses for a period; cash flow is money actually moving in and out
Why is a loan not revenue?	It is borrowed money, not income earned from selling
Definition of overtrading	Expanding faster than the available working capital allows
Net cash flow formula	Total receipts − total payments
Closing balance formula	Opening balance + net cash flow
What becomes next month's opening balance?	This month's closing balance
Can a month have negative net cash flow but a positive closing balance?	Yes — the business is still solvent
Six causes of cash-flow problems	Overtrading, too much stock, late-paying customers, seasonal demand, unexpected costs, over-investment in fixed assets
Four solutions to a cash-flow problem, with their costs	Overdraft (interest), chase debtors (discounts reduce revenue), longer supplier credit (lost discounts), reduce stock (risk of stock-outs)
Gross profit formula	Revenue − cost of sales
Operating profit formula	Gross profit − operating expenses
Profit for the year formula	Operating profit − interest − tax
Retained profit formula	Profit for the year − dividends
Two uses of profit	Paying dividends to owners, or retaining it to finance growth
What does a statement of financial position show?	What the business owns and owes at a single date
Definition of a non-current asset	An asset kept for more than a year, such as premises or equipment
Three examples of current assets	Inventory, trade receivables, cash
Two examples of current liabilities	Trade payables and an overdraft
Capital employed formula	Equity + non-current liabilities
Gross profit margin formula	(gross profit ÷ revenue) × 100
Operating profit margin formula	(operating profit ÷ revenue) × 100
ROCE formula	(operating profit ÷ capital employed) × 100
Which profit figure does this course use for ROCE?	Operating profit
Current ratio formula	Current assets ÷ current liabilities
Acid-test ratio formula	(current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities
Why does the acid test exclude inventory?	Stock may not sell quickly, so it is the stricter test of liquidity
Is a ratio quoted as a percentage?	Liquidity ratios are a ratio or multiple; profitability ratios are percentages
What does a very high current ratio suggest?	Cash or stock sitting idle rather than being invested
Four users of accounts and why	Shareholders (return), lenders (can the loan be repaid), suppliers (will we be paid), employees (job security)
Three limitations of ratio analysis	A ratio means nothing alone, definitions must be consistent, and it ignores morale, brand and market conditions
What should a ratio be compared with?	The same firm over time, or similar firms in the same industry
