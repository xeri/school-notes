Pre-Q Basic	Inflation?	Sustained rise in the general price level	
Pre-Q Basic	Unemployment?	People willing and able to work who cannot find a job	
Pre-Q Basic	Economic growth?	Rise in a country's total output (GDP)	
Pre-Q Basic	Balance of trade?	Difference between a country's exports and imports	
Pre-Q Basic	Recession?	Sustained fall in output: two quarters of falling GDP	
Pre-Q Basic	Business cycle?	Recurring rise and fall of real GDP over time	
Pre-Q Basic	Fiscal policy?	Government use of spending and taxation	
Pre-Q Basic	Monetary policy?	Influencing the economy, usually through interest rates	
Pre-Q Basic	Supply-side policy?	Improving the efficiency of supply	
Pre-Q Basic	Exchange rate?	Price of one currency in terms of another	
Pre-Q Basic	Appreciation (currency)?	Rise in a currency's value against others	
Pre-Q Basic	Depreciation (currency)?	Fall in a currency's value against others	Not asset depreciation
Pre-Q Basic	Externality?	Cost or benefit falling on third parties	
Pre-Q Basic	External cost vs external benefit?	Harm vs gain to an unrelated third party	e.g. pollution vs pollination
Pre-Q Cloze	Social cost = {{c1::private cost}} + {{c2::external cost}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Social benefit = {{c1::private benefit}} + {{c2::external benefit}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Cost-benefit analysis?	Weighing all private and external costs and benefits	
Pre-Q Basic	External constraint?	A limit on business activity the firm cannot control	
Pre-Q Basic	Sustainability?	Meeting our needs without harming future generations' ability to meet theirs	
Pre-Q Basic	Global warming?	Rise in world temperatures from greenhouse gases	
Pre-Q Basic	Pressure group?	Organisation pushing firms to act responsibly	Publicity, protests, boycotts
Pre-Q Basic	Globalisation?	World becoming more interconnected through trade and culture	
Pre-Q Basic	Multinational company (MNC)?	HQ in one country, operations in another	Exporting alone doesn't count
Pre-Q Basic	Protectionism?	Restricting imports to shield domestic producers	
Pre-Q Basic	Tariff?	A tax on imports	
Pre-Q Basic	Quota (trade)?	Physical limit on the quantity imported	
Pre-Q Basic	Embargo?	Ban on trading certain goods or with a country	
Pre-Q Basic	Subsidy (trade)?	Government payment to domestic producers	
Pre-Q Basic	Free trade?	Trade without barriers such as tariffs

Pre-Q Basic	Higher income tax: effect on business?	Lower disposable income cuts sales, especially of luxuries	
Pre-Q Basic	Higher corporate tax: effect on business?	Less profit left to reinvest	
Pre-Q Basic	Higher indirect tax (GST): effect on business?	Higher prices; demand falls for elastic goods	
Pre-Q Basic	Higher interest rates: effect on borrowers?	Loan and overdraft repayments rise	
Pre-Q Basic	Higher interest rates: effect on consumer demand?	Credit-bought goods sell less

Pre-Q Basic	Higher interest rates: business response?	Avoid borrowing; use retained profit; delay investment
