Pre-Q Basic	Own figure rule?	A wrong figure carried forward still earns method marks	
Pre-Q Basic	A ratio needs a figure the case doesn't give?	Say it can't be calculated; don't invent one	
Pre-Q Basic	Best first objective for a new small business?	Survival	
Pre-Q Basic	Realistic promotion for a tiny local business?	Local posters or social media, not TV	
Pre-Q Basic	Realistic research for a new small firm?	Secondary data and a cheap online survey	
Pre-Q Basic	Profitable small firm wants to expand: best advice?	Grow gradually to avoid diseconomies and cash problems
