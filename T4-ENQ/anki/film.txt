Pre-Q Basic	Mise-en-scène?	Everything arranged in front of the camera

Pre-Q Cloze	Camera movement: {{c1::pan}} pivots horizontally; {{c2::tracking shot}} travels with the action; {{c3::zoom}} changes focal length and flattens space		
Pre-Q Basic	Long take: effect?	Refuses to intervene; the duration becomes the content	
Pre-Q Basic	Shallow vs deep focus?	Shallow isolates one plane; deep keeps all context legible	Which lets the viewer choose?
Pre-Q Basic	Rack focus: effect?	Shifts attention without a cut; links two planes	
Pre-Q Basic	Long lens (telephoto): effect?	Flattened, distant image: the look of surveillance	
Pre-Q Basic	Low-key vs high-key lighting?	Dark and high-contrast (threat) vs bright and even	
Pre-Q Basic	Continuity editing?	Invisible, spatially coherent cutting; departures read as disturbance	
Pre-Q Basic	180-degree rule?	Camera stays on one side of the line between subjects	Crossing it disorients
Pre-Q Basic	Withheld reverse shot: effect?	Viewer is denied the answering view or reaction	
Pre-Q Basic	Match cut: effect?	Joins shots by likeness: asserts an equivalence	Not the same as cross-cutting
Pre-Q Basic	Cross-cutting: effect?	Tension, plus forced comparison of two simultaneous strands	Not the same as a match cut
Pre-Q Basic	Elliptical editing?	Part of an action omitted; the viewer must infer it	
Pre-Q Basic	Montage?	Compressed sequence that argues by accumulation	
Pre-Q Basic	Diegetic vs non-diegetic sound?	Characters can hear it vs they cannot (score, voiceover)	
Pre-Q Basic	Sound bridge?	Sound carried across a cut, linking scenes or timelines	
Pre-Q Basic	Silence (film): when does it work?	Only once the film has established a soundscape	
Pre-Q Basic	Restricted vs omniscient narration (film)?	Viewer knows only one character's knowledge (suspense) vs more (dramatic irony)
