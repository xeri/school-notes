Pre-Q Basic	Free indirect discourse?	Character's thoughts in the narrator's 3rd-person voice, no "he thought"	Not direct thought: the voices blend
Pre-Q Basic	Focalisation?	Perspective events are perceived through; may differ from the narrator	
Pre-Q Basic	Unreliable narrator?	Narrator whose account the reader has reason to doubt	
Pre-Q Basic	Narrative distance?	How close the narration sits to a character's mind	
Pre-Q Cloze	Third-person {{c1::limited}} narration builds sympathy and suspense; third-person {{c2::omniscient}} builds authority and irony		One mind vs every mind
Pre-Q Basic	Speech tag (prose)?	Verb/adverb attached to dialogue: the narrator's verdict on each line	
Pre-Q Basic	Direct vs reported speech?	Character's exact words vs narrator's summary (mutes the character)	
Pre-Q Basic	Scene vs summary (prose)?	Moment-by-moment vs compressed; moving into scene signals importance	
Pre-Q Basic	Narrative ellipsis?	Deliberate gap in action or time that the reader must fill	
Pre-Q Basic	In medias res?	Opening mid-action, withholding orientation	
Pre-Q Basic	Plot vs story?	Plot = events as the text arranges them; story = order they happened	
Pre-Q Basic	Theme vs motif?	Theme = argument the text makes; motif = recurring concrete element	One is abstract, one concrete
Pre-Q Basic	Symbol?	Concrete thing standing for an abstraction; meaning invested by context	
Pre-Q Basic	Juxtaposition vs antithesis?	Unlike things placed side by side vs balanced grammatical contrast	
Pre-Q Basic	Implied vs superficial meaning?	Inferred between the lines vs explicitly stated	
Pre-Q Cloze	Stanza lengths: {{c1::couplet}} 2, {{c2::tercet}} 3, {{c3::quatrain}} 4, {{c4::cinquain}} 5, {{c5::sestet}} 6, {{c6::octave}} 8		C-T-Q-C-S-O
Pre-Q Cloze	Fixed forms: sonnet {{c1::14}} lines with a volta; villanelle {{c2::19}} lines with two refrains; ballad in {{c3::quatrains}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Volta?	Turn in a poem's argument; usually where the thesis comes from	
Pre-Q Cloze	Volta position: Petrarchan sonnet at {{c1::line 9}}; Shakespearean sonnet at {{c2::the closing couplet}}		Octave + sestet vs 3 quatrains + couplet
Pre-Q Basic	Free verse?	No regular metre or rhyme; line breaks still do the work	
Pre-Q Cloze	Metrical feet: iamb {{c1::da-DUM}}, trochee {{c2::DUM-da}}, anapaest {{c3::da-da-DUM}}, dactyl {{c4::DUM-da-da}}, spondee {{c5::DUM-DUM}}		Give the stress pattern
Pre-Q Basic	Iambic pentameter?	Five iambs per line; the metre closest to speech	
Pre-Q Basic	Enjambment: effect?	Momentum, or a double reading of the line's last word	
Pre-Q Basic	End-stopping: effect?	Finality or flatness; the sound of a verdict	
Pre-Q Basic	Caesura?	Pause inside a line; can split it into opposed halves	
Pre-Q Basic	Refrain: effect?	Same words mean something different on each return	
Pre-Q Basic	Simile vs metaphor?	Simile admits the comparison (like/as); metaphor asserts identity	
Pre-Q Basic	Personification?	Human qualities given to a non-living thing (never to a human)	
Pre-Q Basic	Pathetic fallacy?	Weather or landscape reflecting human emotion	Not the same as personification
Pre-Q Basic	Metonymy vs synecdoche?	Associated thing (the crown) vs part for the whole (hands)	"Invisible hands" is which?
Pre-Q Basic	Oxymoron vs paradox?	Contradiction within one phrase vs seemingly contradictory statement that reveals a truth	
Pre-Q Basic	Synaesthesia?	One sense described in the terms of another	
Pre-Q Basic	Connotation vs denotation?	Associations beyond the literal vs dictionary meaning	
Pre-Q Basic	Register (language)?	Level and variety of language chosen (formal vs colloquial etc.)	
Pre-Q Basic	Semantic field: how to use?	Quote 3 words from one domain and name it: shows the writer's attitude	
Pre-Q Cloze	Repetition: {{c1::anaphora}} at the start of lines; {{c2::epistrophe}} at the end; {{c3::epizeuxis}} immediately (<i>fool, fool, fool</i>)		A-E-E
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Asyndeton}} omits conjunctions to speed a list; {{c2::polysyndeton}} piles them up to slow it		
Pre-Q Basic	Assonance vs consonance?	Repeated vowel sounds vs repeated consonant sounds anywhere in words	
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Plosives}} (p, b, t, d, k, g) sound abrupt; {{c2::fricatives}} (f, v, th) sound breathy		Name the consonant class
Pre-Q Basic	Sibilance: effect?	Hush, whisper or menace; moving air or water	
Pre-Q Basic	Half rhyme: effect?	Expected chime denied: unease, incompleteness	
Pre-Q Basic	Tone vs mood?	Tone = the voice's attitude; mood = the feeling of the text's world	Often confused: voice vs world
Pre-Q Cloze	Tonal shift signals: change in {{c1::sentence length}}; change of {{c2::tense or person}}; a word from a new {{c3::register}}; the first {{c4::question or imperative}}		4 signals
Pre-Q Cloze	Before analysing, ask: {{c1::who speaks}}? {{c2::what is the situation}}? {{c3::where does it change}}?		
Pre-Q Cloze	Analytical sentence: name the device → {{c1::describe its mechanism}} → "and the effect is to…" → {{c2::the implied meaning}}		The middle step is the one weak answers skip
Pre-Q Cloze	Commentary paragraph order: {{c1::structure}} → {{c2::imagery}} → {{c3::diction and sound}} → {{c4::allusion}} → the close		S-I-D-A
