Pre-Q Basic	Thesis?	One arguable sentence stating an informed position on the question	
Pre-Q Basic	Topic vs theme?	Topic = one word (race); theme = what the text claims about it	
Pre-Q Basic	Point sentence?	Paragraph's opening claim: supports the thesis with a reason	
Pre-Q Basic	Rationale (introduction)?	Brief preview of the sequence of points to come	
Pre-Q Basic	Hook?	Opening broad, universal idea about human experience	Narrowed to the text in the next sentence
Pre-Q Basic	Summation?	Thesis restated in the conclusion, now as demonstrated	
Pre-Q Basic	Contemporary connect?	The text's moral or philosophical relevance to the reader	
Pre-Q Cloze	SICK quotation: {{c1::Selective}}, {{c2::Integrated}}, {{c3::Contextualised}}, {{c4::Knowledge of purpose}}		S-I-C-K
Pre-Q Basic	Discourse marker?	Word signalling the logical link between sentences or paragraphs	
Pre-Q Basic	Concession?	Acknowledging a valid opposing point before answering it	
Pre-Q Basic	Qualification (argument)?	Narrowing a claim to the conditions where it holds	
Pre-Q Basic	Author-intention verb?	Verb giving the writer a purpose (exposes / undercuts / satirises)	
Pre-Q Basic	Integration (quotation)?	Placing the quotation inside your own sentence	
Pre-Q Basic	Paraphrase vs analysis?	Paraphrase restates; analysis explains why the words were chosen	
Pre-Q Basic	Comma splice?	Two independent clauses joined by only a comma	
Pre-Q Basic	Assertion adverb: when a fault?	When "clearly" or "obviously" replaces the argument	
Pre-Q Basic	Analysis (paragraph move)?	Shows how conventions imply meaning beyond the surface	
Pre-Q Basic	Explanation (paragraph move)?	How the evidence proves the point, at theme level	
Pre-Q Cloze	Unpack the question: {{c1::command word}}, {{c2::focus noun}}, {{c3::limits}}		3 parts
Pre-Q Basic	Question contains "and/or"?	Choose, and say which in the thesis	
Pre-Q Basic	Question quotes a critic: best response?	Agree with qualification	Better than agreeing or disagreeing outright
Pre-Q Basic	"Discuss" demands?	A reasoned case acknowledging more than one side, then a position	
Pre-Q Basic	"Explore" demands?	Follow an idea through the text, including its complications	
Pre-Q Basic	"Examine the ways" demands?	At least three distinct methods, each with its effect	"Ways" is plural
Pre-Q Basic	"To what extent" demands?	A judgement of degree; the concession earns the mark	
Pre-Q Basic	"Comment on how, and with what effects" demands?	The method AND its effect on the reader	
Pre-Q Cloze	Introduction: {{c1::hook}} → {{c2::transition}} → {{c3::rationale}} → {{c4::thesis}}		H-T-R-T
Pre-Q Basic	Where does the thesis go?	Last sentence of the introduction, opened by "Ultimately" or "Thus"	
Pre-Q Basic	Passage-based introduction: extra move?	1–2 sentences placing the extract, without retelling the plot	
Pre-Q Cloze	Body paragraph: {{c1::point}}, {{c2::evidence}}, {{c3::analysis}}, {{c4::explanation}}, {{c5::link}}		P-E-A-E-L
Pre-Q Cloze	P.E.E. weighting: point + evidence {{c1::25%}}, exploration {{c2::75%}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Paragraph levels: {{c1::language}} → {{c2::literary/filmic element}} → {{c3::theme}}		Middle = character, structure or setting
Pre-Q Basic	"How?" failure?	Device linked straight to theme, skipping character/structure	
Pre-Q Basic	"So what?" failure?	Analysis stops at character and never reaches theme	
Pre-Q Basic	Colon trick (point sentence)?	After a vague word, add a colon and say exactly what you mean	
Pre-Q Cloze	Three ways to integrate a quotation: {{c1::dialogue tag}}, {{c2::colon}}, {{c3::flow on}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Ellipsis vs square brackets in a quotation?	Ellipsis = words cut; brackets = words altered	
Pre-Q Cloze	Conclusion: {{c1::summation}}, then {{c2::contemporary connect}}; never {{c3::new evidence}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Historical context should be contemporary to?	The writer (a Jacobean audience for a Jacobean play)	Not today's news
Pre-Q Basic	Tense for the text vs for history?	Present for the text; past for history	
Pre-Q Cloze	Proofread for: {{c1::comma splices}}, {{c2::contractions}}, {{c3::tense slippage}}, {{c4::misspelt names or titles}}		4 killers
Pre-Q Basic	Replace "this quote shows" with?	Name the device and describe its mechanism	
Pre-Q Basic	Target essay length for one hour?	About 650 words; four body paragraphs	
Pre-Q Basic	Running out of time mid-paragraph?	Stop and write the conclusion
