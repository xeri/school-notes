Pre-Q Basic	Natural resource?	Anything in nature people can use (water, soil, minerals)	
Pre-Q Basic	Resource depletion?	Using a resource faster than it is replaced	
Pre-Q Basic	Conservation?	Protecting and managing resources and environments so they last	
Pre-Q Basic	Sustainability?	Meeting present needs without stopping future generations meeting theirs	
Pre-Q Basic	Ecological footprint?	Land and sea area needed to supply resources and absorb waste	
Pre-Q Basic	Circular economy?	Reduce, reuse, repair and recycle so materials stay in use	
Pre-Q Basic	Water stress?	Water demand approaches or exceeds supply	
Pre-Q Basic	Physical water scarcity?	Not enough water exists (deserts, drought)	
Pre-Q Basic	Economic water scarcity?	Water exists but people can't afford the infrastructure	not physical scarcity
Pre-Q Basic	Water security?	Reliable access to enough safe water	
Pre-Q Basic	Aquifer (water supply)?	Underground rock layer storing groundwater	
Pre-Q Basic	Desalination?	Removing salt from sea water to make fresh water	
Pre-Q Basic	Pollution haven?	Country with weak environmental rules that attracts polluting industry	
Pre-Q Basic	Externality?	Cost or benefit of an activity falling on third parties	
Pre-Q Basic	Kaitiakitanga?	Māori guardianship and protection of the environment	
Pre-Q Basic	Environmental impact assessment?	Study of a project's environmental effects before approval
