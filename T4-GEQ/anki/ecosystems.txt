Pre-Q Basic	Ecosystem?	Living organisms interacting with their non-living environment as one system	
Pre-Q Basic	Biotic vs abiotic	Biotic = living parts; abiotic = non-living parts such as climate	
Pre-Q Basic	Biome?	Large ecosystem with similar climate and vegetation	
Pre-Q Basic	Producer (ecosystem)?	Green plant making food by photosynthesis	
Pre-Q Basic	Decomposer?	Fungi and bacteria returning nutrients from dead matter	
Pre-Q Basic	Biodiversity?	Variety of species in an area	
Pre-Q Basic	Nutrient cycle?	Movement of nutrients between soil, biomass and litter	
Pre-Q Basic	Biomass?	Total mass of living material in an ecosystem	
Pre-Q Basic	Leaching?	Soluble nutrients washed down out of the topsoil	
Pre-Q Basic	Latosol?	Deep red leached rainforest soil, poor in nutrients	
Pre-Q Basic	Deforestation?	Clearance of forest for other land uses	
Pre-Q Basic	Desertification?	Degradation of dry land into desert-like conditions	
Pre-Q Basic	Hot desert?	Under about 250 mm rain a year; hot days, large daily range
