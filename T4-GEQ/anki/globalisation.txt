Pre-Q Basic	Primary sector (economy)?	Extracting or growing raw materials (farming, mining, fishing)	
Pre-Q Basic	Secondary sector (economy)?	Processing raw materials and manufacturing goods	
Pre-Q Basic	Tertiary sector (economy)?	Providing services (retail, health, transport)	
Pre-Q Basic	Quaternary sector (economy)?	Knowledge, research and information services (ICT, R&D)	
Pre-Q Basic	Employment structure?	% of the workforce in each sector	
Pre-Q Basic	Economic transition?	Change in the sectors' importance as a country develops	
Pre-Q Basic	Clark–Fisher model?	Employment shifts primary → secondary → tertiary as a country develops	
Pre-Q Basic	Globalisation?	Increasing interconnection of economies, cultures and people worldwide	
Pre-Q Basic	Transnational corporation (TNC)?	Company with headquarters in one country, operations in others	
Pre-Q Basic	Multiplier effect?	Initial investment creates jobs and spending, which create more jobs	
Pre-Q Basic	Negative multiplier?	A closure causes a downward spiral of lost jobs and spending	
Pre-Q Basic	Foreign direct investment (FDI)?	A company investing in businesses or factories in another country	
Pre-Q Basic	Outsourcing (offshoring)?	Moving production or services to another firm or country	
Pre-Q Basic	Global supply chain?	Network of firms and countries that make and deliver a product	
Pre-Q Basic	Global shift?	Manufacturing moving from HICs to NICs and LICs	
Pre-Q Basic	Interdependence?	Countries relying on each other for goods, markets and investment	
Pre-Q Basic	Containerisation?	Shipping goods in standard steel containers, cutting transport costs	
Pre-Q Basic	Trade liberalisation?	Falling tariffs and quotas through the WTO and free-trade deals	
Pre-Q Basic	Host country vs home country (TNC)?	Host: where the TNC operates. Home: where its headquarters is	
Pre-Q Basic	Cultural homogenisation?	Local cultures becoming alike through global brands

Pre-Q Basic	Clark–Fisher post-industrial stage?	Tertiary dominant; secondary falling; quaternary growing	e.g. UK
Pre-Q Cloze	Economic transition chain: farm {{c1::mechanisation}} frees labour, {{c2::factories}} draw workers, rising {{c3::incomes}} raise service demand		3 steps
Pre-Q Basic	Why does deindustrialisation happen in HICs?	Labour costs rise, so routine manufacturing relocates abroad	
Pre-Q Basic	Why do most LIC workers work in the primary sector?	Little education or capital; subsistence farming	
Pre-Q Basic	Clark–Fisher model: limitations?	UK-based; some skip stages (India) or stay primary exporters	
Pre-Q Cloze	Globalisation drivers: {{c1::containerisation}}, {{c2::ICT}}, {{c3::trade liberalisation}}, {{c4::TNCs}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	TNC location pattern?	HQ and research at home; factories where cheap or near markets	
Pre-Q Cloze	Why TNCs locate in a country: cheap {{c1::labour}}, {{c2::raw materials}}, a large {{c3::market}}, government {{c4::incentives}}		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	TNC benefits to host: {{c1::jobs}}, {{c2::skills}}, {{c3::technology}}, {{c4::tax revenue}}, multiplier effect		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	TNC costs to host: profits {{c1::leak abroad}}, {{c2::low wages}}, {{c3::pollution}}, sudden {{c4::closure}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	TNC: advantages for the home country?	Profits return; skilled HQ jobs	
Pre-Q Basic	TNC: main disadvantage for the home country?	Manufacturing jobs lost abroad
