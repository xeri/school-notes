Pre-Q Basic	Tourism?	Travel away from home for leisure or business, staying at least one night	
Pre-Q Basic	Domestic vs international tourism?	Domestic: within your own country. International: to another country	
Pre-Q Basic	Mass tourism?	Large numbers on package holidays to resorts	
Pre-Q Basic	Ecotourism?	Small-scale, nature-based, low-impact tourism benefiting locals and conservation	
Pre-Q Basic	Cultural (heritage) tourism?	Visiting historic sites, festivals and ways of life	
Pre-Q Basic	Adventure tourism?	Active, risky activities (bungy, trekking)	
Pre-Q Basic	Butler model?	Tourist area life cycle: growth, stagnation, then decline or rejuvenation	
Pre-Q Basic	Butler model: big idea?	Unmanaged tourism destroys the qualities that first attracted visitors	
Pre-Q Basic	Butler model: when should management start?	Involvement and development stages, before the damage is done	
Pre-Q Basic	Carrying capacity (tourism)?	Maximum visitors before environment or experience is damaged	think limits
Pre-Q Basic	Leakage (tourism)?	Tourist spending lost to foreign-owned airlines and hotels	think foreign ownership
Pre-Q Basic	Sustainable tourism?	Meets visitor and host needs while protecting environment and culture	
Pre-Q Basic	Community-based tourism?	Tourism owned and run by locals, keeping income local

Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam's Hội An: management?	Ticketing funds restoration; pedestrian-only streets

Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam's Sơn Đoòng?	World's largest cave passage: capped, costly ecotourism	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam tourism: negative economic impacts?	Leakage; seasonal low pay; reliance on China and Korea	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam tourism leakage: why bad?	Spending goes to foreign firms and big conglomerates; little reaches local families	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam: why did COVID-19 hurt tourism workers so badly?	Low-paid, insecure jobs with no safety net; arrivals fell to almost nothing	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam: why is relying on China and Korea risky?	A dispute, slowdown or pandemic there hits Vietnam directly	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam's Hạ Long Bay: why does pollution matter?	Fewer fish for fishers; a dirtier bay deters the visitors it depends on	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam's Sa Pa: effect of hillside hotel building?	Forest and terraces lost; landslides in heavy rain; the attraction built over	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam's Sa Pa child street-sellers: long-term effect?	They miss school, so stay trapped in the cycle of poverty	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam's Hội An: effect of overcrowding?	Residents move out; the old town becomes shops (a museum town)	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam tourism: big idea of its negative impacts?	Unmanaged tourism destroys the attractions it depends on	
Pre-Q Basic	Vietnam: why is tourism management struggling?	Visitors grow faster than management; industry also pollutes Hạ Long	
Pre-Q Basic	Queenstown (tourism): main problems?	Unaffordable worker housing; congestion; collapse during COVID-19	
Pre-Q Basic	Why do countries keep investing in tourism?	Foreign exchange; low-skill jobs; income for remote regions
