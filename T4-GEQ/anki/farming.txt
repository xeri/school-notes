Pre-Q Basic	Food shortage?	Not enough food for an active, healthy life	
Pre-Q Basic	Famine?	Severe, widespread food shortage causing mass hunger and death	
Pre-Q Basic	Food security?	Reliable access to enough affordable, nutritious food	
Pre-Q Basic	Malnutrition?	Ill health from a diet lacking enough food or nutrients	
Pre-Q Basic	Green Revolution?	1960s high-yield varieties, fertiliser, pesticides and irrigation raising yields	
Pre-Q Basic	Genetically modified (GM) crops?	Crops altered for pest or drought resistance and higher yields

Pre-Q Basic	Salinisation?	Salts left in soil when irrigation water evaporates	think arid irrigated land
Pre-Q Basic	Pugging?	Soil compaction by cattle on wet ground	
Pre-Q Basic	Desertification (farming cause)?	Overgrazing and over-cultivation strip semi-arid land of vegetation	
Pre-Q Basic	Carrying capacity (farming)?	Maximum stocking rate land supports without degradation
