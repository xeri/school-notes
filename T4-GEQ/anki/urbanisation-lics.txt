Pre-Q Basic	Over-urbanisation?	Urban population grows faster than urban jobs and infrastructure	
Pre-Q Basic	Squatter settlement (informal settlement)?	Self-built housing without legal land title or planning permission	
Pre-Q Basic	Informal economy?	Unregistered, untaxed work (street trading, waste picking)	
Pre-Q Basic	Formal sector?	Registered, taxed work with contracts and legal protection	
Pre-Q Basic	Latin American city model?	LIC/MIC city model: rich near the centre, squatters on the edge	
Pre-Q Basic	Commercial spine?	Boulevard from the CBD lined with offices, shops and élite housing	Latin American model
Pre-Q Basic	Zone of maturity?	Older, established housing with full services	Latin American model
Pre-Q Basic	Zone of in-situ accretion?	Modest housing being improved bit by bit; partial services	Latin American model
Pre-Q Basic	Disamenity sector?	Worst land (steep, flood-prone, polluted), occupied by the poorest	
Pre-Q Basic	Self-help scheme?	Residents improve their own homes with supplied materials, title or loans	
Pre-Q Basic	Site-and-service scheme?	Authorities provide serviced plots; residents build their own homes	not the same as self-help
Pre-Q Basic	In-situ upgrading?	Improving a slum with services and titles without moving residents	
Pre-Q Basic	Micro-credit?	Small loans to poor people to start or expand a business	
Pre-Q Basic	RDP housing?	Free basic houses under South Africa's Reconstruction and Development Programme (1994)	
Pre-Q Basic	New town (counter-magnet)?	Planned town built to divert growth from a large city

Pre-Q Basic	Rural-to-urban migration: benefits to the city?	Cheap workforce; enterprise in the informal sector

Pre-Q Basic	Rural-to-urban migration: benefits to rural areas?	Remittances; less pressure on land	
Pre-Q Basic	Rural-to-urban migration: costs to rural areas?	Young men leave; farm labour shortage; services close	
Pre-Q Cloze	Latin American city model: {{c1::Griffin and Ford}}, {{c2::1980}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Latin American city model: where do rich and poor live?	Rich: spine near the centre. Poor: edge squatter settlements	
Pre-Q Basic	Why is the Latin American model the reverse of Burgess?	Wealth falls outwards; unwanted land is on the edge	compare where the rich live
Pre-Q Basic	Latin American city model: limitations?	Fits Africa and Asia poorly; central slums and edge gated estates
