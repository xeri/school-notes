Pre-Q Basic	Resource?	Naturally occurring substance or feature people can use	
Pre-Q Basic	Renewable resource?	Replenished naturally on a human timescale	
Pre-Q Basic	Non-renewable resource?	Finite stock used up faster than it forms	
Pre-Q Basic	What does "renewable" describe?	Rate of replacement, not absence of environmental impact	think of a dam
Pre-Q Basic	Fossil fuel?	Coal, oil or gas formed from ancient organisms	
Pre-Q Basic	Primary energy?	Energy in its raw form (coal, crude oil, sunlight)	
Pre-Q Basic	Energy mix?	Combination of sources a country uses to meet demand	
Pre-Q Basic	Energy security?	Reliable, affordable energy without dependence on unstable suppliers	think fuel imports
Pre-Q Basic	Energy conservation?	Using less energy through efficiency and changed behaviour	
Pre-Q Basic	Peak oil?	Point when oil production peaks, then declines	think a production curve
Pre-Q Basic	Intermittent energy source?	Produces power only some of the time (wind, solar)	think night and calm days
Pre-Q Basic	Fracking?	Cracking shale rock with high-pressure water to release gas	think shale gas
Pre-Q Basic	Nuclear power?	Electricity from the heat of splitting uranium atoms	
Pre-Q Basic	Biofuel?	Fuel made from plant material or organic waste	
Pre-Q Basic	Geothermal energy?	Heat from the Earth's crust, usable in volcanic areas	
Pre-Q Basic	Hydroelectric power (HEP)?	Electricity from water falling through turbines	
Pre-Q Basic	Solar power?	Electricity from sunlight via photovoltaic cells, or solar water heating	
Pre-Q Basic	Fuelwood?	Wood burned for cooking and heat; renewable only if replanted

Pre-Q Cloze	Renewable energy: {{c1::HEP}}, {{c2::geothermal}}, {{c3::wind}}, {{c4::solar}}, tidal, wave, biofuel		4 gaps
Pre-Q Basic	Renewable energy with environmental impact: examples?	Dams flood valleys; turbines kill birds	
Pre-Q Basic	Coal: advantages?	Large reserves; cheap; reliable	
Pre-Q Basic	Coal: disadvantages?	Most CO₂ per unit of energy; air pollution and acid rain	
Pre-Q Basic	Oil: advantages?	Energy-dense and transportable; fuels nearly all transport	
Pre-Q Basic	Oil: disadvantages?	Finite; volatile price; spills; CO₂	
Pre-Q Basic	Natural gas: main advantage?	Cleanest fossil fuel; quick back-up for renewables	
Pre-Q Basic	Fracking: risks?	Groundwater contamination; small earthquakes	
Pre-Q Basic	Nuclear power: advantages?	No CO₂ in generation; huge, reliable output	
Pre-Q Basic	Nuclear power: disadvantages?	Costly and slow to build; radioactive waste; accident risk	
Pre-Q Cloze	Nuclear accidents: {{c1::Chernobyl}} (1986) and {{c2::Fukushima}} (2011)		
Pre-Q Basic	HEP: advantages?	Renewable and controllable; stores water; flood control	
Pre-Q Basic	HEP: disadvantages?	Floods valleys and displaces people; traps sediment; blocks fish	3 effects on river and people
Pre-Q Basic	Geothermal: drawbacks?	Volcanic areas only; releases gases; subsidence	
Pre-Q Basic	Wind power: drawbacks?	Intermittent; noise and visual impact; bird deaths	
Pre-Q Basic	Solar power: drawbacks?	None at night; less in cloud and winter	
Pre-Q Basic	Why does solar power suit many LICs?	Sunny; works off-grid; free once installed	
Pre-Q Basic	Tidal power: drawbacks?	Very costly; few sites; damages estuaries	
Pre-Q Basic	Biofuel: main drawback?	Competes with food crops for land	
Pre-Q Cloze	Fuelwood problems: {{c1::deforestation}}, {{c2::desertification}}, {{c3::indoor smoke}}, {{c4::collection time}}		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	About {{c1::40%}} of the world's people rely on fuelwood
