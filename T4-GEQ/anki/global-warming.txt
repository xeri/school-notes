#deck:Geography::46 Global warming and climate change
#tags:geography global-warming
Pre-Q Basic	Climate change?	Long-term changes in temperature, rainfall, wind and the climate system	
Pre-Q Basic	Global warming?	Rise in Earth's average surface temperature from rising greenhouse gases	
Pre-Q Basic	Greenhouse effect?	Natural trapping of heat by atmospheric gases, keeping Earth warm	
Pre-Q Basic	Enhanced greenhouse effect?	Extra heat trapped because human emissions raise greenhouse gases	natural vs human
Pre-Q Basic	Anthropogenic?	Caused by human activity	
Pre-Q Basic	Anthropogenic pollution?	Pollution caused directly or indirectly by humans	
Pre-Q Basic	Mitigation (climate)?	Reducing emissions: tackling the cause	
Pre-Q Basic	Adaptation (climate)?	Adjusting to climate change: living with the effects	
Pre-Q Basic	Mitigation vs adaptation: example of each?	Mitigation: renewable energy. Adaptation: sea walls	
Pre-Q Basic	Carbon footprint?	Greenhouse gas emitted directly or indirectly by a person, firm or product	
Pre-Q Basic	Carbon sink?	Store absorbing more carbon than it releases (forests, oceans)	think forests
Pre-Q Basic	Net zero?	Emissions cut as far as possible, the rest balanced by removals	
Pre-Q Basic	Carbon cycle?	Movement of carbon between atmosphere, oceans, living things and rocks	
Pre-Q Basic	Ocean acidification?	Fall in ocean pH as seawater absorbs extra CO₂	think pH
Pre-Q Basic	Coral bleaching?	Heat-stressed corals expel their algae, turn white and often die	
Pre-Q Basic	Climate migrant?	Someone forced from home by the effects of climate change	
Pre-Q Basic	Atoll?	Ring-shaped coral island built round a sinking volcanic island	
Pre-Q Basic	Photochemical smog?	Haze formed when sunlight reacts with vehicle exhaust	worse under inversions
Pre-Q Basic	Acid rain?	Rain made acidic by sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides	
Pre-Q Basic	Ozone depletion?	CFCs thin the ozone layer, letting in more UV	
Pre-Q Basic	Carbon capture and storage?	Trapping CO₂ from power stations or industry and storing it underground	
Pre-Q Basic	Global warming vs ozone depletion?	Warming: heat trapped by greenhouse gases. Ozone depletion: UV shield thinned by CFCs	commonly confused
