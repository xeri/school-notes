Pre-Q Basic	Urbanisation?	Rising proportion of a population living in urban areas	
Pre-Q Basic	Urban growth?	Rising urban population or built-up area	
Pre-Q Basic	Urbanisation vs urban growth?	Urbanisation: a proportion. Urban growth: numbers or area	which one is a %?
Pre-Q Basic	Megacity?	Urban area of over 10 million people	
Pre-Q Basic	Suburbanisation?	Housing spreads onto the city edge; residents commute back in	
Pre-Q Basic	Commuting?	Daily journey from home to work and back	
Pre-Q Basic	Commuter (dormitory) village?	Village whose working residents travel to the city daily	
Pre-Q Basic	Urban sprawl?	Uncontrolled, low-density spread of a city onto rural land	
Pre-Q Basic	Rural–urban fringe?	Transition zone where the built-up area meets countryside	
Pre-Q Basic	Greenfield site?	Undeveloped land on the edge of a built-up area	
Pre-Q Basic	Urban shadow?	Rural area beyond the fringe shaped by the nearby city	
Pre-Q Basic	Land banking?	Holding land undeveloped until rezoned and its value rises	think speculators
Pre-Q Basic	Counter-urbanisation?	Movement out of cities to small towns and rural areas	
Pre-Q Basic	Suburbanisation vs counter-urbanisation?	Suburbanisation: to the city edge. Counter-urbanisation: out of the city entirely	
Pre-Q Basic	Green belt?	Protected countryside ring round a city where building is controlled	
Pre-Q Basic	Urban growth boundary?	Line beyond which urban development is not allowed	
Pre-Q Basic	Rural Urban Boundary (RUB)?	Auckland's growth limit line in the 2016 Unitary Plan	
Pre-Q Basic	Intensification?	More homes inside the existing urban area, not outwards	
Pre-Q Basic	Infill?	New homes on spare land between or behind existing buildings	not the same as brownfield reuse
Pre-Q Basic	Compact city?	City growing up and in, near jobs, services and transit	
Pre-Q Basic	Inner-city decay?	Decline of inner-city economy, housing and environment after industry closes	
Pre-Q Basic	Deindustrialisation?	Decline of manufacturing industry in an area	
Pre-Q Basic	Comprehensive redevelopment?	Demolishing a whole area of old buildings and rebuilding it	not the same as renewal
Pre-Q Basic	Urban renewal?	Improving existing housing and environment without demolishing	
Pre-Q Basic	Urban regeneration?	Public agencies attract private investment to transform a run-down area	
Pre-Q Basic	Gentrification?	Richer people buy and improve inner-city homes, displacing poorer residents	
Pre-Q Basic	Re-urbanisation?	People moving back into the inner city, usually after regeneration

Pre-Q Basic	Britain's early urbanisation: 2 causes?	Agricultural revolution freed labour; industrial revolution built factories
