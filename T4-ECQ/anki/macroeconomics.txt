Pre-Q Cloze	Circular flow: households supply {{c1::factors of production}} to firms and receive {{c2::incomes}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Macro objectives: {{c1::growth}}, {{c2::employment}}, {{c3::stable prices}}, {{c4::balance of payments stability}}, {{c5::less poverty and inequality}}, {{c6::protecting the environment}}		6 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Growth is measured by % change in {{c1::real GDP}}; inflation by % change in the {{c2::CPI}}; BoP by the {{c3::current account as % of GDP}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Gross domestic product (GDP)?	Value of goods and services produced in a country per period	
Pre-Q Cloze	GDP = {{c1::C}} + {{c2::I}} + {{c3::G}} + ({{c4::X − M}})		Consumption first
Pre-Q Basic	Economic growth?	Annual % change in real GDP	
Pre-Q Cloze	Causes of growth: {{c1::factor endowments}}, {{c2::labour force}}, {{c3::productivity}}, {{c4::investment}}		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Drawbacks of growth: {{c1::technological unemployment}}, {{c2::resource depletion}}, {{c3::pollution}}, {{c4::inflation}}, {{c5::inequality}}		5 items
Pre-Q Basic	Recession?	Real GDP falls for two successive quarters or more	Count quarters
Pre-Q Basic	Nominal GDP?	GDP at current prices	
Pre-Q Basic	Real GDP?	GDP with the effect of inflation removed	
Pre-Q Cloze	Real GDP = {{c1::nominal GDP}} ÷ ({{c2::price index}} ÷ 100)		
Pre-Q Basic	Real GDP per head?	Real GDP divided by population	
Pre-Q Basic	Why is real GDP per head best for living standards?	Removes inflation and population growth	
Pre-Q Basic	Economic welfare?	Level of prosperity and quality of living standards	
Pre-Q Cloze	GDP ignores: {{c1::non-market activity}}, {{c2::sustainability}}, {{c3::inequality}}, {{c4::what is produced}}, {{c5::externalities}}		5 items
Pre-Q Basic	Economic cycle?	Recurring ups and downs of real GDP around its trend	
Pre-Q Cloze	Economic cycle order: {{c1::growth}}, {{c2::boom}}, {{c3::recession}}, {{c4::slump}}, {{c5::recovery}}		5 stages
Pre-Q Basic	V-shaped vs U-shaped recession	V: short with quick recovery. U: slower recovery	
Pre-Q Basic	Inflation?	General, sustained rise in the average price level	
Pre-Q Basic	Hyperinflation?	Very rapid inflation, often over 50% a month	
Pre-Q Basic	Consumer price index (CPI)?	Weighted price of a basket of typical household spending	
Pre-Q Cloze	CPI = {{c1::weighted average price this year}} ÷ {{c2::weighted average price in base year}} × 100		Base year = 100
Pre-Q Cloze	Inflation rate = ({{c1::new CPI − old CPI}}) ÷ {{c2::old CPI}} × 100		
Pre-Q Basic	CPI above 100 means?	Prices higher than in the base year	
Pre-Q Basic	How to read a CPI value as % above base year?	Subtract 100	Not this year's inflation
Pre-Q Cloze	Uses of the CPI: {{c1::economic indicator}}, {{c2::price deflator}}, {{c3::indexation}}		3 items
Pre-Q Basic	Demand-pull inflation?	Aggregate demand rises faster than output	
Pre-Q Basic	Cost-push inflation?	Rising production costs passed on as higher prices	
Pre-Q Basic	Imported inflation?	Dearer imports (e.g. weaker currency) raise domestic prices	
Pre-Q Basic	Inflation: savers vs borrowers	Savers lose; borrowers gain	
Pre-Q Basic	Deflation?	Sustained fall in the general price level	
Pre-Q Basic	Disinflation?	Inflation rate slowing; prices still rising	Not deflation
Pre-Q Basic	Benign vs malign deflation	Benign: supply rises. Malign: demand falls	
Pre-Q Cloze	Against inflation: {{c1::raise interest rates}}, {{c2::cut spending or raise taxes}}, {{c3::supply-side policy}}		3 policy types
Pre-Q Basic	Labour force?	Working-age people willing and able to work	Two key words
Pre-Q Cloze	Unemployment rate = {{c1::number unemployed}} ÷ {{c2::labour force}} × 100		Not population
Pre-Q Basic	Claimant count?	Number claiming unemployment-related benefits	
Pre-Q Basic	Labour force survey?	Household survey using the ILO definition of unemployed	
Pre-Q Basic	Full employment?	Low unemployment; job seekers find work fairly quickly	
Pre-Q Basic	Cyclical unemployment?	Low demand in a recession	
Pre-Q Basic	Structural unemployment?	Industry decline leaves workers with wrong skills or location	
Pre-Q Basic	Frictional unemployment?	Time between leaving one job and starting another	
Pre-Q Basic	Seasonal unemployment?	Jobs exist only at certain times of year	
Pre-Q Cloze	Costs of unemployment: {{c1::lost income}}, {{c2::lower GDP}}, {{c3::welfare spending}}, {{c4::social problems}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Fiscal policy?	Varying government spending and taxation to influence demand	
Pre-Q Cloze	Principles of a good tax: {{c1::equity}}, {{c2::non-distortionary}}, {{c3::certainty}}, {{c4::convenience}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Direct vs indirect tax	Direct: on income or profit. Indirect: on spending	
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Progressive}} tax: proportion taken rises as income rises		
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Proportional}} tax: same proportion at every income		
Pre-Q Cloze	{{c1::Regressive}} tax: proportion taken falls as income rises		
Pre-Q Basic	Why is GST regressive?	Low earners spend more of their income, so more is taxed	
Pre-Q Basic	Effective (average) tax rate?	Total tax ÷ income	
Pre-Q Basic	Marginal tax rate?	Rate on the last dollar earned	
Pre-Q Basic	How to calculate income tax with brackets?	Tax each slice at its own rate, then add	
Pre-Q Cloze	Budget balance = {{c1::government revenue}} − {{c2::government spending}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Budget deficit vs surplus	Deficit: spending > revenue. Surplus: revenue > spending	
Pre-Q Basic	Expansionary vs contractionary fiscal policy	Spend more or tax less vs spend less or tax more	
Pre-Q Basic	Monetary policy?	Central bank control of interest rates and money supply	
Pre-Q Cloze	Higher interest rate (OCR) → {{c1::dearer borrowing}} → {{c2::less spending and investment}} → {{c3::lower aggregate demand}} → lower inflation		
Pre-Q Basic	Supply-side policy?	Policy raising productive capacity (aggregate supply)	
Pre-Q Cloze	Supply-side measures: {{c1::education and training}}, {{c2::tax incentives}}, {{c3::deregulation}}, {{c4::competition policy}}, {{c5::privatisation}}		5 items
Pre-Q Basic	Full employment vs stable prices: conflict?	Tight labour market raises wages: wage–price spiral	
Pre-Q Basic	Growth vs balance of payments: conflict?	Rising incomes raise spending on imports	
Pre-Q Basic	Full employment vs balance of payments: conflict?	More income from jobs is spent on imports	
Pre-Q Basic	Demand boost: spare capacity vs full capacity	Spare: output rises. Full: prices rise	
Pre-Q Basic	Transfer payments?	Government payments with nothing produced in return (benefits, pensions)	
Pre-Q Basic	Excise duty?	Fixed tax per unit, e.g. on alcohol and tobacco	
Pre-Q Basic	User charges?	Fees paid to use a public service, e.g. road tolls	
Pre-Q Basic	Budget surplus?	Government revenue > spending	Opposite of deficit
Pre-Q Basic	K-shaped recovery?	Some sectors recover quickly while others keep declining	Two arms of the K
Pre-Q Basic	Dependent population?	People outside the labour force: children, students, retired	
Pre-Q Basic	Under-utilisation (labour)?	Part-time workers who want more hours	Missed by the unemployment rate
Pre-Q Basic	Regional policy?	Subsidies for firms locating in high-unemployment areas	Fixes structural unemployment
