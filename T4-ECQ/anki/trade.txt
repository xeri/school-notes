Pre-Q Basic	Globalisation?	World becoming more interconnected through trade and cultural exchange

Pre-Q Basic	Globalisation: 2 benefits to producers	Bigger markets; locate where inputs are cheaper

Pre-Q Basic	Over-dependence risk in trade?	A fall in world price or demand hits the whole economy

Pre-Q Basic	Absolute advantage?	Producing more of a good with the same resources	
Pre-Q Basic	Comparative advantage?	Lower opportunity cost of producing a good	Not the same as absolute advantage
Pre-Q Basic	How to find the opportunity cost of 1 unit of a good?	Other good given up ÷ units of this good gained	Compare countries afterwards

Pre-Q Cloze	MNC drawbacks to host: {{c1::local firms close}}, {{c2::worker exploitation}}, {{c3::profits sent home}}, {{c4::transfer pricing}}		4 items

Pre-Q Basic	Free trade?	Trade with no barriers

Pre-Q Basic	Tariff?	Tax on imports	
Pre-Q Basic	Import quota?	Limit on the quantity imported

Pre-Q Basic	Tariff vs quota for government	Tariff raises revenue; quota doesn't

Pre-Q Basic	Sunset industry argument?	Avoid sudden mass unemployment as an industry declines

Pre-Q Basic	Free trade agreement (FTA)?	Agreement removing tariffs and easing trade between countries	
Pre-Q Basic	Customs union?	No internal tariffs plus a common external tariff	
Pre-Q Basic	Exchange rate?	Value of one currency compared with another	
Pre-Q Basic	Appreciation?	Currency rises; buys more foreign currency	
Pre-Q Basic	Depreciation?	Currency falls; buys less foreign currency	
Pre-Q Basic	Convert home currency to foreign at a given rate?	Multiply by the rate	Rate quoted as 1 home = x foreign
Pre-Q Basic	Convert foreign currency to home at a given rate?	Divide by the rate	Rate quoted as 1 home = x foreign
Pre-Q Basic	Floating exchange rate?	Set by demand and supply only	
Pre-Q Basic	Fixed exchange rate?	Pegged to an anchor currency	
Pre-Q Basic	Managed exchange rate?	Central bank steers it around a preferred rate	
Pre-Q Basic	Who demands a currency?	Foreigners buying its goods, services or assets	
Pre-Q Basic	Who supplies a currency?	Residents buying imports or foreign assets	Opposite of who demands it
Pre-Q Basic	Demand for a currency rises: diagram?	Demand shifts right; rate rises (appreciation)	
Pre-Q Basic	More imports bought: currency diagram?	Supply of the currency shifts right; it depreciates	
Pre-Q Basic	Higher relative interest rates: currency?	Appreciates	
Pre-Q Basic	Higher relative inflation: currency?	Depreciates	
Pre-Q Basic	Fixed rate held above equilibrium acts as?	A minimum price; central bank buys the excess supply	Think price floor
Pre-Q Basic	Floating exchange rate: 2 advantages	Adjusts automatically; no reserves needed	
Pre-Q Basic	Floating exchange rate: 2 disadvantages	Volatility; imported inflation	
Pre-Q Basic	Fixed exchange rate: 2 advantages	Certainty for traders; inflation discipline	
Pre-Q Basic	Fixed exchange rate: 2 disadvantages	Needs large reserves; interest rates tied to the rate	
Pre-Q Basic	Depreciation: export and import prices?	Exports cheaper abroad; imports dearer at home	
Pre-Q Basic	Depreciation: effect on inflation?	Rises (imported inflation)	
Pre-Q Basic	Appreciation: jobs and growth?	Export jobs lost; growth slows
