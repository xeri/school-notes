Pre-Q Basic	Economic growth vs economic development	Growth: more real output. Development: better quality of life	
Pre-Q Basic	Economic development?	Improvement in quality of life and living standards	
Pre-Q Basic	Can growth happen without development?	Yes; gains wasted or kept by a wealthy few	
Pre-Q Basic	Real GDP per head: main weakness as an indicator?	An average; hides how income is distributed	
Pre-Q Cloze	Other development indicators: {{c1::life expectancy}}, {{c2::literacy rate}}, {{c3::infant mortality}}, {{c4::access to clean water}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Human Development Index (HDI)?	UN composite measure of living standards, from 0 to 1	
Pre-Q Cloze	HDI components: {{c1::income}} (GNI per head), {{c2::education}} (years of schooling), {{c3::health}} (life expectancy)		3 items
Pre-Q Cloze	HDI limits: ignores {{c1::income distribution}}, {{c2::gender equality and rights}}, {{c3::the environment}}, {{c4::cultural differences}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Gini coefficient?	Measure of how evenly income is distributed	
Pre-Q Cloze	Gini coefficient {{c1::0}} = perfect equality; {{c2::1}} = perfect inequality		
Pre-Q Basic	Lorenz curve?	Cumulative % of income against cumulative % of population, poorest first	
Pre-Q Cloze	Gini from a Lorenz curve = {{c1::A}} ÷ ({{c2::A + B}})	A: between equality line and curve. B: below curve	Areas, not lengths
Pre-Q Basic	Human Poverty Index (HPI)?	UN index counting deprivation directly	
Pre-Q Cloze	The {{c1::Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI)}} replaced the HPI; it covers health, education and {{c2::living standards}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	MEDC = {{c1::More Economically Developed Country}}; LEDC = {{c2::Less Economically Developed Country}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	LEDC features: {{c1::low-output farming}}, {{c2::few firms}}, {{c3::poor transport}}, {{c4::little education and healthcare}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Emerging economy?	Country developing rapidly; newly industrialised	
Pre-Q Basic	Limits of MEDC and LEDC labels?	Two boxes for a spectrum; hide within-country gaps	
Pre-Q Cloze	Reasons for low development: {{c1::dependence on agriculture}}, {{c2::lack of capital}}, {{c3::poor education and health}}, {{c4::poor infrastructure}}, {{c5::high population growth}}, {{c6::unstable government}}		6 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Vicious circle: low income → low {{c1::saving}} → low {{c2::investment}} → low {{c3::productivity}} → low income		
Pre-Q Cloze	Within-country gaps: {{c1::urban vs rural}}, {{c2::region vs region}}, {{c3::formal vs informal work}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Birth rate vs death rate	Live births vs deaths, per 1000 people per year	
Pre-Q Cloze	Natural increase = {{c1::birth rate}} − {{c2::death rate}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Net migration = {{c1::immigration}} − {{c2::emigration}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Population change = {{c1::natural increase}} + {{c2::net migration}}		
Pre-Q Basic	How to turn a rate per 1000 into number of people?	Divide by 1000, multiply by population	
Pre-Q Cloze	Birth rate falls with: {{c1::fewer child deaths}}, {{c2::contraception}}, {{c3::female employment}}, {{c4::later marriage}}		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Death rate falls with: {{c1::better living standards}}, {{c2::medical advances}}, {{c3::healthcare access}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Immigration benefits: {{c1::fills skill gaps}}, {{c2::more tax revenue}}, {{c3::entrepreneurship}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Brain drain?	Skilled workers emigrating for higher pay abroad	
Pre-Q Cloze	4 population distributions: {{c1::age}}, {{c2::sex}}, {{c3::geographic}}, {{c4::occupational}}		
Pre-Q Basic	LEDC population pyramid shape?	Wide base, narrow top	
Pre-Q Basic	MEDC population pyramid shape?	Narrow base; bulge in middle and upper ages	
Pre-Q Cloze	Reading a pyramid: base shows {{c1::birth rate}}; top shows {{c2::life expectancy}}; bulges show {{c3::booms, wars or migration}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Dependency ratio = ({{c1::aged 0–14 + aged 65+}}) ÷ {{c2::aged 15–64}} × 100		Dependants over workers
Pre-Q Cloze	Ageing population effects: higher {{c1::pension and healthcare costs}}, smaller {{c2::workforce}}, heavier {{c3::tax burden on workers}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Optimum population?	Level at which output per head is maximised	
Pre-Q Basic	Over-population?	Too many people for the available resources	
Pre-Q Basic	Under-population?	Too few workers to use resources fully	
Pre-Q Cloze	Cutting population growth: {{c1::education}}, {{c2::contraception}}, {{c3::removing child incentives}}, {{c4::limiting immigration}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Poverty?	Lacking resources for a minimum standard of living	
Pre-Q Basic	Extreme (absolute) poverty?	Cannot meet basic needs for food, water and shelter	
Pre-Q Basic	Moderate poverty?	Basic needs met but no resources to improve life	
Pre-Q Basic	Relative poverty?	Lacking what others in the same country normally have	
Pre-Q Cloze	Causes of poverty in every country: {{c1::unemployment}}, {{c2::low wages}}, {{c3::illness or disability}}, {{c4::age}}		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Poverty cycle: low income → poor {{c1::food and healthcare}} → {{c2::sickness}} → low {{c3::energy and schooling}} → few jobs → low income		
Pre-Q Cloze	Anti-poverty policies: {{c1::growth}}, {{c2::education}}, {{c3::state benefits}}, {{c4::progressive tax}}, {{c5::minimum wage}}		5 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Judge development policies on: {{c1::access}}, {{c2::quality}}, {{c3::implementation capacity}}, {{c4::time before benefits}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Income poverty?	Household income below the official poverty line	
Pre-Q Basic	Occupational distribution?	What work people do, by sector	MEDCs mostly services
