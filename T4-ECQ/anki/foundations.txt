Pre-Q Cloze	Economics studies the {{c1::production}}, {{c2::distribution}} and {{c3::consumption}} of goods and services		P-D-C
Pre-Q Basic	Scarcity?	Unlimited wants but limited means to satisfy them	
Pre-Q Basic	Need?	Something people must have to survive	
Pre-Q Basic	Want?	Something people would like but can survive without	
Pre-Q Cloze	3 limited means: {{c1::time}}, {{c2::money or income}}, {{c3::skill and knowledge}}		3 items
Pre-Q Cloze	3 basic economic questions: {{c1::what}} to produce, {{c2::how}} to produce, {{c3::for whom}} to produce		
Pre-Q Basic	Opportunity cost?	The next best alternative forgone	Only one alternative counts
Pre-Q Basic	Goods vs services	Goods are physical items; services are done for you	
Pre-Q Basic	Free good?	Plentiful and costless, e.g. sunshine	
Pre-Q Basic	Economic good?	Scarce, so it has a cost	
Pre-Q Basic	Consumer good?	Good for private use by households	
Pre-Q Basic	Durable vs non-durable consumer goods	Durable last (cars); non-durable are used up (food)	
Pre-Q Basic	Capital (producer) good?	Man-made good used to make other goods and services	
Pre-Q Cloze	3 types of resource: {{c1::natural}}, {{c2::human}}, {{c3::man-made}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Factors of production?	Scarce resources used to produce goods and services	
Pre-Q Cloze	4 factors of production: {{c1::land}}, {{c2::labour}}, {{c3::capital}}, {{c4::enterprise}}		L-L-C-E
Pre-Q Cloze	Factor rewards: land earns {{c1::rent}}, labour {{c2::wages}}, capital {{c3::interest}}, enterprise {{c4::profit}}		R-W-I-P
Pre-Q Basic	Land (factor of production)?	Place of business activity plus all natural resources	
Pre-Q Basic	Labour (factor of production)?	Human physical and mental effort	
Pre-Q Basic	Capital (factor of production)?	Man-made productive equipment, not money	
Pre-Q Basic	Enterprise?	Organising the other factors and taking the risk	
Pre-Q Basic	Factor mobility?	How easily a factor moves between uses or places	
Pre-Q Basic	Occupational vs geographical mobility	Moving between jobs or uses vs moving between places	
Pre-Q Basic	Public good?	Good that is non-excludable and non-rival	
Pre-Q Basic	Non-excludable?	Non-payers cannot be stopped from benefiting	
Pre-Q Basic	Non-rival?	One person's use does not reduce what is left for others	
Pre-Q Cloze	Public good examples: {{c1::street lighting}}, {{c2::lighthouses}}, {{c3::national defence}}		3 items
Pre-Q Basic	Merit good?	Beneficial good consumers undervalue, so it is under-consumed	
Pre-Q Basic	Demerit good?	Harmful good consumers overvalue, so it is over-consumed	
Pre-Q Basic	Why are merit goods not public goods?	Merit goods are excludable and rival	Think school places
Pre-Q Basic	Specialisation?	Concentrating on one product or task	
Pre-Q Basic	Division of labour?	Splitting production into tasks, each done by a specialist	
Pre-Q Cloze	Division of labour advantages: {{c1::higher productivity}}, {{c2::less wasted time}}, {{c3::easier use of specialised machinery}}		3 items
Pre-Q Cloze	Division of labour disadvantages: {{c1::boredom cuts quality}}, {{c2::low motivation}}, {{c3::one absence can stop production}}		3 items
Pre-Q Basic	Derived demand?	Demand for something because of demand for another product	Think lithium and phones
Pre-Q Basic	Law of diminishing returns?	Add one factor to fixed others: extra output eventually falls	Think more café staff, same space
Pre-Q Basic	Production possibility curve (PPC)?	Maximum combinations of two goods using all resources efficiently	
Pre-Q Basic	Point inside the PPC?	Inefficient; both goods can rise with no opportunity cost	
Pre-Q Basic	Point outside the PPC?	Unattainable with current resources and technology	
Pre-Q Basic	How to find opportunity cost from a PPC?	Units of one good given up for the units gained	
Pre-Q Basic	PPC bowed outwards (concave)?	Increasing opportunity cost	
Pre-Q Basic	Straight-line PPC?	Constant opportunity cost	
Pre-Q Basic	Steeper section of a PPC?	Higher opportunity cost of the horizontal-axis good	
Pre-Q Basic	Movement along a PPC?	Reallocating resources between the two goods; not a shift	
Pre-Q Basic	Outward PPC shift means?	Economic growth: more productive capacity	
Pre-Q Cloze	PPC shifts out with: {{c1::higher productivity}}, {{c2::more capital or labour}}, {{c3::innovation}}, {{c4::new natural resources}}		4 items
Pre-Q Cloze	PPC shifts in with: {{c1::natural disasters}}, {{c2::large emigration}}, {{c3::war}}, {{c4::loss of capital}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Recession on a PPC?	Point moves inside the curve; the curve stays put	Movement or shift?
Pre-Q Basic	More capital goods now: PPC effect?	Faster outward shifts later; less consumption now	Think opportunity cost
Pre-Q Basic	Economic system?	How an economy answers the three basic questions	
Pre-Q Cloze	4 economic systems: {{c1::free market}}, {{c2::command (planned)}}, {{c3::mixed}}, {{c4::traditional}}		4 items
Pre-Q Basic	Free market allocates resources by?	Price, through demand and supply	
Pre-Q Cloze	Free market advantages: {{c1::efficiency}}, {{c2::freedom of choice}}, {{c3::incentive to work}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Free market disadvantages: {{c1::environmental damage}}, {{c2::inequality}}, {{c3::no public goods}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Command economy?	Government decides everything, often through five-year plans	
Pre-Q Cloze	Command economy advantages: {{c1::less waste}}, {{c2::social equality}}, {{c3::economies of scale}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Command economy: 2 disadvantages	No freedom of choice; no incentive to work harder	
Pre-Q Basic	Mixed economy?	Markets for most goods; state provides essentials and merit goods	
Pre-Q Basic	Traditional economy?	Decisions made by custom and tradition	
Pre-Q Basic	Private sector?	Owned by individuals or firms, mainly for profit	
Pre-Q Basic	Public sector?	Owned by government, mainly to provide a service	
Pre-Q Cloze	3 functions of the price mechanism: {{c1::signalling}}, {{c2::incentive}}, {{c3::rationing}}		S-I-R
Pre-Q Basic	Signalling function of price?	Rising price tells producers buyers want more	
Pre-Q Basic	Incentive function of price?	Higher price makes supplying more profitable	
Pre-Q Basic	Rationing function of price?	Scarce good goes to those willing to pay most	
Pre-Q Cloze	Price mechanism limits: {{c1::ignores external costs}}, {{c2::no public goods}}, {{c3::too few merit goods}}, {{c4::allocates by ability to pay}}		4 items
