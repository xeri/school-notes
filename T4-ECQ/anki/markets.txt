Pre-Q Basic	Demand?	Quantity consumers are willing and able to buy at a range of prices	Two key words
Pre-Q Basic	Quantity demanded (Qd)?	Amount consumers buy at one particular price	
Pre-Q Basic	Market demand?	Sum of every individual's demand at each price	
Pre-Q Basic	Ceteris paribus?	All other factors stay the same	
Pre-Q Basic	Law of demand?	Price rises, quantity demanded falls (ceteris paribus)	
Pre-Q Basic	Why does the demand curve slope down?	At lower prices consumers are more able and willing to buy	
Pre-Q Basic	Movement along a demand curve is caused by?	A change in the good's own price	
Pre-Q Basic	Shift of a demand curve is caused by?	A condition of demand (any non-price factor)	
Pre-Q Basic	Increase in demand?	Curve shifts right: more demanded at every price	
Pre-Q Basic	Extension vs contraction of demand	Extension: price fall raises Qd. Contraction: price rise cuts Qd	
Pre-Q Cloze	Conditions of demand: {{c1::tastes}}, {{c2::disposable income}}, {{c3::price of substitutes}}, {{c4::price of complements}}, {{c5::population}}, {{c6::advertising}}, {{c7::expectations}}		7 items
Pre-Q Basic	Substitute's price rises: demand for the good?	Rises (shifts right)	
Pre-Q Basic	Complement's price falls: demand for the good?	Rises (shifts right)	Used together
Pre-Q Basic	Normal good?	Demand rises as income rises	
Pre-Q Basic	Inferior good?	Demand falls as income rises	Think bus travel
Pre-Q Basic	Luxury good?	Demand rises more than proportionately with income	
Pre-Q Basic	Substitutes?	Goods used instead of each other, e.g. tea and coffee	
Pre-Q Basic	Complements?	Goods used together, e.g. bread and butter	
Pre-Q Basic	Why is "a price rise decreases demand" wrong?	It cuts quantity demanded (movement), not demand (shift)	Movement vs shift
Pre-Q Basic	Supply?	Quantity producers are willing and able to sell at a range of prices	
Pre-Q Basic	Quantity supplied (Qs)?	Amount supplied at one particular price	
Pre-Q Basic	Market supply?	Sum of all firms' supply in the market	
Pre-Q Basic	Law of supply?	Price rises, quantity supplied rises (ceteris paribus)	
Pre-Q Basic	Why does the supply curve slope up?	Higher price makes supplying more profitable	
Pre-Q Basic	Related goods (supply)?	Goods made with the same inputs	
Pre-Q Basic	Related good's price rises: supply of this good?	Falls; resources switch to the related good	Think rolls and loaves
Pre-Q Basic	Price vs cost	Price: what the buyer pays. Cost: what producing takes	
Pre-Q Cloze	Conditions of supply: {{c1::costs of production}}, {{c2::technology}}, {{c3::indirect taxes}}, {{c4::subsidies}}, {{c5::tariffs on inputs}}, {{c6::number of firms}}, {{c7::seasons}}, {{c8::price of related goods}}		8 items
Pre-Q Basic	Indirect tax raised: supply?	Decreases (shifts left)	
Pre-Q Basic	Subsidy given to producers: supply?	Increases (shifts right)	
Pre-Q Basic	New technology: supply?	Increases (shifts right)	
Pre-Q Basic	Increase in supply vs increase in quantity supplied	Supply: curve shifts right. Qs: movement from a price rise	
Pre-Q Basic	How to build market demand from individual demand?	Add each consumer's quantity at every price	Horizontal summation
Pre-Q Basic	Equilibrium price?	Price where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied	
Pre-Q Basic	Excess demand (shortage)?	Qd exceeds Qs, at a price below equilibrium	
Pre-Q Basic	Excess supply (surplus)?	Qs exceeds Qd, at a price above equilibrium	
Pre-Q Basic	Shortage: how does price adjust?	Price is bid up; Qs rises and Qd falls	
Pre-Q Basic	Surplus: how does price adjust?	Price falls; Qd rises and Qs falls	
Pre-Q Basic	Demand increases: equilibrium price and quantity?	Both rise	
Pre-Q Basic	Demand decreases: equilibrium price and quantity?	Both fall	
Pre-Q Basic	Supply increases: equilibrium price and quantity?	Price falls; quantity rises	
Pre-Q Basic	Supply decreases: equilibrium price and quantity?	Price rises; quantity falls	
Pre-Q Basic	Demand and supply both increase?	Quantity rises; price depends on the bigger shift	One outcome is ambiguous
Pre-Q Basic	Two steps to explain a demand or supply shift?	Shift one curve, then movement along the other	
Pre-Q Basic	How to find equilibrium from Qd and Qs equations?	Set Qd = Qs and solve for P; then find Q	Check in both equations
Pre-Q Cloze	Price-setting factors: {{c1::PED}}, {{c2::type of product}}, {{c3::the customers}}, {{c4::rest of the marketing mix}}, {{c5::brand image}}		5 items
Pre-Q Basic	Cost-plus pricing?	Unit cost plus a fixed percentage mark-up	
Pre-Q Basic	Competitor pricing?	Setting price around rivals' prices	
Pre-Q Basic	Price discrimination?	Different prices for different groups of customers	
Pre-Q Basic	Contribution pricing?	Cover variable cost, plus a contribution to fixed costs	
Pre-Q Basic	Penetration pricing?	Low entry price, raised later	
Pre-Q Basic	Price skimming?	High launch price, lowered over time	Opposite of penetration
Pre-Q Basic	Psychological pricing?	Prices like 99c that feel cheaper	
Pre-Q Basic	Predatory pricing?	Very low price (even below cost) to force rivals out	
Pre-Q Basic	Price war?	Rival firms repeatedly cutting prices to win market share	
Pre-Q Basic	Demand schedule?	Table of prices and quantities demanded, ceteris paribus	
Pre-Q Basic	Supply schedule?	Table of prices and quantities supplied
