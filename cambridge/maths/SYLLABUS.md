## 1. Pure Mathematics 1 (Paper 1)

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**1.1 Quadratics** ([notes](notes/p1-quadratics.md))
- Complete the square, and use it to find the vertex and the range.
- Use the discriminant: the number of real roots, and when a line is a tangent to a curve.
- Solve quadratic equations and inequalities, simultaneous equations with one linear and one quadratic, and equations that reduce to a quadratic.

**1.2 Functions** ([notes](notes/p1-functions.md))
- Domain, range, one-one functions, inverse and composite functions.
- The graph of a function and its inverse (reflection in y = x).
- Transformations of graphs: translations, reflections and stretches, and their combinations.

**1.3 Coordinate geometry** ([notes](notes/p1-coordinate-geometry.md))
- Lines: gradient, length, midpoint, parallel and perpendicular lines, the equation of a line.
- Circles: the equation, its centre and radius, tangents and intersections.
- Where a line meets a curve.

**1.4 Circular measure** ([notes](notes/p1-circular-measure.md))
- Radians and converting to and from degrees.
- Arc length and sector area, and problems that combine them with triangles.

**1.5 Trigonometry** ([notes](notes/p1-trigonometry.md))
- Graphs of sine, cosine and tangent, and their transformations; exact values.
- Inverse trig functions and their principal values.
- The identities for tan and for sin² + cos²; solving trig equations in a given interval.

**1.6 Series** ([notes](notes/p1-series.md))
- The binomial expansion for a positive whole-number power.
- Arithmetic and geometric progressions: the nth term and the sum; the sum to infinity of a convergent geometric progression.

**1.7 Differentiation** ([notes](notes/p1-differentiation.md))
- The gradient of a curve; differentiating powers; the chain rule.
- Tangents and normals; increasing and decreasing functions; stationary points and their nature; connected rates of change.

**1.8 Integration** ([notes](notes/p1-integration.md))
- Integration as the reverse of differentiation; finding a curve from its gradient and a point.
- Definite integrals; the area under and between curves; volumes of revolution; simple improper integrals.

## 5. Probability & Statistics 1 (Paper 5)

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**5.1 Representation of data** ([notes](notes/s1-data.md))
- Stem-and-leaf diagrams, box-and-whisker plots, histograms and cumulative frequency graphs.
- Measures of average and spread, including the standard deviation from raw data, from summary statistics and from coded data.

**5.2 Permutations and combinations** ([notes](notes/s1-permutations-combinations.md))
- Arrangements, with and without repetition and restrictions; selections.

**5.3 Probability** ([notes](notes/s1-probability.md))
- Mutually exclusive and independent events; conditional probability; tree and Venn diagrams.

**5.4 Discrete random variables** ([notes](notes/s1-discrete-random-variables.md))
- Probability distributions, expectation and variance.
- The binomial and geometric distributions.

**5.5 The normal distribution** ([notes](notes/s1-normal-distribution.md))
- Its properties; standardising and using tables; finding an unknown mean or standard deviation.
- The normal approximation to the binomial, with a continuity correction.
