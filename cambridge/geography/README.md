## Exam technique

**Each paper is 90 minutes for 60 marks:** about 45 minutes for Section A and 45 for Section B.

**Section A (resources):**
- **Describe** what the resource shows: the overall pattern, then the highest and lowest values with figures and units, then the exception.
- **Explain** with processes, not more description.
- Quote the resource every time.

**Section B (essay):**
- Plan for two minutes: the argument, two or three developed points, a case study for each, and the judgement.
- Case studies need the place, when it happened, figures, and what it shows.
- "To what extent" needs a clear answer that says what it depends on (scale, wealth, time, management).

**This loses marks:**
- describing without figures, or explaining without processes;
- case studies with no place, date or numbers;
- essays with no judgement.

## How to study it

- Pair every concept with a case, and every case with the concept it shows. That pairing is what the essays mark.
- Learn processes as chains and landforms as labelled sketches, drawn from memory.
- Fill in each topic's slots in [case-studies.md](case-studies.md) as you go.
