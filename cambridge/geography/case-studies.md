# Geography case studies (AS)

The case studies each AS topic needs, as slots to fill. Check each slot against the syllabus *(to verify)*, choose a case, then write it up with the place, when, figures, effects, responses and what it shows. The Pre-Q cases are in [the Pre-Q case-study sheet](../../T4-GEQ/CASE-STUDIES.md) and can be reused where they fit.
