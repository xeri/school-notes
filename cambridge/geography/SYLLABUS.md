## 1. Hydrology, river processes and hazards

*Outline, to verify against the new syllabus.*

**1.3 River processes and landforms** ([notes](notes/rivers.md))

**1.4 River flood hazards and their management** ([notes](notes/rivers.md))
- The causes and effects of floods; hard and soft management.
