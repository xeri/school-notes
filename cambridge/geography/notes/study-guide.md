# Geography study guide (AS, Oct/Nov 2027)

What to study first and how, through the AS year. The papers and every topic are on the [syllabus page](../SYLLABUS.md).

## The exam at a glance

| Paper | What you do | Marks | Time | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Physical: resource questions on all three topics, then one topic's structured question and essay | 60 | 1 h 30 | 1.5 minutes a mark |
| **2** | Human: the same shape | 60 | 1 h 30 | 1.5 minutes a mark |

Section A tests reading resources (maps, graphs, photos) and explaining them. Section B's essay rewards a concept, a detailed case study and a judgement together. Technique: [exam technique](exam-technique.md).

## How to study geography at AS

- **Pair every concept with a case,** and every case with the concept it shows. That pairing is what the essays mark.
- **Processes as chains, landforms as labelled sketches,** drawn from memory.
- **Case-study detail:** the place, the date, two or three figures, and what it shows.
