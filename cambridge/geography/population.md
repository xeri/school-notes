- **4.1 Natural increase:** Birth and death rates and what affects them.
- **4.2 Demographic transition:** The model, its stages and its limits; age–sex structures.
- **4.3 Population and resources:** Overpopulation, underpopulation and optimum population; food security.
- **4.4 Managing population change:** Pro-natalist and anti-natalist policies.
- **4.5 Migration:** Types and causes of migration; its impacts on the source and the destination.
