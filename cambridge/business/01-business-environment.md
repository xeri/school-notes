# Business and its environment

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 1, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **1.1 Enterprise:** What businesses do: inputs to outputs, adding value; the entrepreneur's qualities and role; social enterprise.
- **1.2 Business structure:** The primary, secondary and tertiary sectors; the public and private sectors; legal structures and limited liability.
- **1.3 Size of business:** Ways to measure size; the strengths and weaknesses of small businesses; internal and external growth.
- **1.4 Business objectives:** Mission, aims and SMART objectives; corporate social responsibility; why objectives change.
- **1.5 Stakeholders in a business:** Internal and external stakeholders, their roles and interests; conflicts between stakeholder objectives.
