## Exam technique

**Paper 1, at just under 2 minutes a mark.** About 35 minutes for the four short answers (usually a define part and an explain part), then 40 minutes for the essay (an analyse part and an evaluate part *(to verify)*).

**Paper 2, at 45 minutes a question.**
- Read each case once for the story and once for the numbers, and mark what you can use.
- The parts run from 1-mark identify and 3-mark explain or calculate up to an 8-mark analysis and a 12-mark evaluation *(to verify)*.

**How the marks are given** *(assessment objectives to verify)*:
- **Knowledge:** the right term or theory.
- **Application:** using this business's product, numbers and situation.
- **Analysis:** a developed chain of consequences.
- **Evaluation:** a justified judgement or recommendation for this business.
- An answer with no application stays in the lowest levels, however good its theory.

**This loses marks:**
- points that would fit any business;
- mixing up profit and cash, or revenue and profit;
- calculations with no working or no unit;
- a conclusion that only repeats the arguments.

## How to study it

- Definitions first, then straight to application: "what does this mean for *this* business?", in a line or two.
- Know every calculation cold: the formula, the method, the unit, and what the answer means for the business.
- For every essay plan: two developed points, each applied to the case, then a judgement that says what it depends on.
