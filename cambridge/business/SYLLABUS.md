## 1. Business and its environment

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**1.1 Enterprise** ([notes](notes/01-business-environment.md))
- What businesses do: inputs to outputs, adding value; the entrepreneur's qualities and role; social enterprise.

**1.2 Business structure** ([notes](notes/01-business-environment.md))
- The primary, secondary and tertiary sectors; the public and private sectors; legal structures and limited liability.

**1.3 Size of business** ([notes](notes/01-business-environment.md))
- Ways to measure size; the strengths and weaknesses of small businesses; internal and external growth.

**1.4 Business objectives** ([notes](notes/01-business-environment.md))
- Mission, aims and SMART objectives; corporate social responsibility; why objectives change.

**1.5 Stakeholders in a business** ([notes](notes/01-business-environment.md))
- Internal and external stakeholders, their roles and interests; conflicts between stakeholder objectives.

## 2. Human resource management

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**2.1 Human resource management** ([notes](notes/02-hrm.md))
- What HRM is for; workforce planning; recruitment and selection; training; dismissal and redundancy.

**2.2 Motivation** ([notes](notes/02-hrm.md))
- The main motivation theories and what they mean for a business; financial and non-financial methods.

**2.3 Management** ([notes](notes/02-hrm.md))
- What managers do; leadership styles and when each suits.

## 3. Marketing

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**3.1 The nature of marketing** ([notes](notes/03-marketing.md))
- The role of marketing; mass and niche markets; market size, share and growth; adding value through marketing.

**3.2 Market research** ([notes](notes/03-marketing.md))
- Primary and secondary research; qualitative and quantitative data; sampling methods; interpreting results.

**3.3 The marketing mix** ([notes](notes/03-marketing.md))
- Product, price, promotion and place, and how they fit together and support the objectives.

## 4. Operations management

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**4.1 The nature of operations** ([notes](notes/04-operations.md))
- Inputs, process and outputs; efficiency, effectiveness and productivity; job, batch and flow production.

**4.2 Inventory management** ([notes](notes/04-operations.md))
- Why inventory is held; the costs of too much and too little; inventory control charts; just in time.

**4.3 Capacity utilisation and outsourcing** ([notes](notes/04-operations.md))
- Measuring capacity utilisation; under- and over-utilisation; the benefits and risks of outsourcing.

## 5. Finance and accounting

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**5.1 Business finance** ([notes](notes/05-finance.md))
- Why businesses need finance (start-up, working capital, investment); why cash and profit differ.

**5.2 Sources of finance** ([notes](notes/05-finance.md))
- Internal and external sources, short and long term; choosing the right source.

**5.3 Forecasting and managing cash flows** ([notes](notes/05-finance.md))
- Cash-flow forecasts and ways to improve cash flow.

**5.4 Costs** ([notes](notes/05-finance.md))
- Fixed, variable, direct and indirect costs; contribution and break-even analysis.

**5.5 Budgets** ([notes](notes/05-finance.md))
- What budgets are for; incremental, flexible and zero-based budgets; variance analysis.
