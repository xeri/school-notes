# Business calculations (AS)

Every calculation AS Business can ask for, as a checklist. Each gets its formula, a worked example and its traps when its topic is written up. The Pre-Q versions are in [the Pre-Q calculations sheet](../../T4-BUQ/CALCULATIONS.md).

| 3 | Market size, market share and market growth (%) | ☐ |
| 3 | Price elasticity of demand and its effect on revenue *(to verify)* | ☐ |
| 4 | Labour productivity | ☐ |
| 4 | Capacity utilisation | ☐ |
| 4 | Reading an inventory control chart: buffer stock, reorder level, lead time | ☐ |
| 5 | Revenue, costs and profit | ☐ |
| 5 | Contribution, break-even output, margin of safety | ☐ |
| 5 | Cash-flow forecast: net cash flow, opening and closing balances | ☐ |
| 5 | Budget variances: favourable and adverse | ☐ |
| all | Percentage change and percentage share | ☐ |
