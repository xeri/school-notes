# Business study guide (AS, Oct/Nov 2027)

What to study first and how, through the AS year. The papers and every topic are on the [syllabus page](../SYLLABUS.md).

## The exam at a glance

| Paper | What you do | Marks | Time | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | A: four 5-mark short-answer questions. B: one 20-mark essay from two | 40 | 1 h 15 | under 2 minutes a mark |
| **2** | two compulsory data-response questions on unseen businesses | 60 | 1 h 30 | 45 minutes a question |

Most marks go to **applying** an idea to the business in front of you, then **analysing** its consequences and **evaluating** what the business should do. A point that would fit any business earns little. Technique: [exam technique](exam-technique.md).

## How to study business at AS

- **Definitions first, then straight to application.** Once a term is known, practise "what does this mean for *this* business?" in a line or two.
- **Calculations cold:** formula, method, unit, and what the answer means for the business.
- **For every essay plan:** two developed points, each applied to the case, then a judgement that says what it depends on.
