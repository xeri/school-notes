# Exam technique: AS Business

How each AS paper works and how its marks are given. Details marked *(to verify)* need checking against the syllabus and a recent mark scheme.

## Paper 1: Business Concepts 1

1 hour 15 minutes for 40 marks: just under 2 minutes a mark.

| Section | What it is | Time |
|---|---|---|
| A | four short-answer questions, 5 marks each, usually a define/identify part and an explain part *(to verify)* | 35 min |
| B | one essay from a choice of two, 20 marks, usually an analyse part and an evaluate part *(to verify)* | 40 min |

## Paper 2: Business Concepts 2

1 hour 30 minutes for 60 marks: two compulsory data-response questions, each on an unseen business, so 45 minutes each. One revision guide gives each question six parts, running from 1-mark identify and 3-mark explain or calculate parts up to an 8-mark analysis and a 12-mark evaluation *(to verify)*.

- **Read the case once for the story and once for the numbers;** mark everything you can use (figures, objectives, problems, people).
- **Every answer names the business and uses its details.**

## How the marks are given

The assessment objectives are knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation *(names and weightings to verify)*:
- **Knowledge:** the correct term or theory.
- **Application:** using the case: its product, its numbers, its situation.
- **Analysis:** a developed chain of consequences ("…which means…, so…").
- **Evaluation:** a justified judgement or recommendation that says what it depends on.

The long parts are marked in levels. An answer without application stays in the lowest levels however good its theory.

## Command words in this subject

| Word | In business |
|---|---|
| Define · Identify | the term or factor, precisely and briefly |
| Explain | the meaning plus why or how, applied to the case |
| Calculate | show the formula and working; give the unit |
| Analyse | develop the consequences for this business, step by step |
| Evaluate · Discuss · Recommend | both sides, then a justified judgement for this business |

## This loses marks

- Generic points that never mention the business in the case.
- Confusing profit and cash, or revenue and profit.
- Calculations with no working, or with no unit.
- A conclusion that repeats the arguments instead of making a judgement.
