# Exam technique: AS English Language

How the AS papers work and how marks are given. Details marked *(to verify)* need checking against the syllabus and a recent mark scheme.

## The papers

- **Paper 1, Reading:** 2 hours 15 minutes for 50 marks, two compulsory questions on unseen texts, about 25 marks each *(to verify)*. Allow about 65 minutes a question, including reading time.
- **Paper 2, Writing:** 2 hours for 50 marks: one compulsory question, then one from a choice *(to verify)*. About an hour each.

## Reading tasks

- **Directed response:** write the new text in the form asked, for the audience asked, using the source's material. Don't copy whole phrases from it.
- **Analysis:** start from the text's audience, purpose and form, then explain how its language and structure serve them. Quote briefly and comment on the effect.
- **Comparison:** compare as you go (similarities and differences point by point) rather than writing about one text and then the other.

## Writing tasks

- **Form first:** the conventions of the form asked for (a speech, a review, an article, a story…).
- **Register and audience:** a consistent voice that suits the reader.
- **Reflective commentary:** explain your main choices of language and structure, and the effect you intended. Don't summarise what you wrote.

## How the marks are given

Reading is marked on understanding and analysis; writing on how well the piece suits its form, audience and purpose, and on its accuracy and range *(assessment objectives to verify)*. Both are marked in levels.

## This loses marks

- Listing features ("there is a metaphor") with no effect explained.
- Writing in the wrong form, or with the wrong register for the audience.
- A commentary that retells the piece instead of explaining its choices.
