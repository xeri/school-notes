# English Language study guide (AS, Oct/Nov 2027)

What to study first and how, through the AS year. The papers and every topic are on the [syllabus page](../SYLLABUS.md).

## The exam at a glance

| Paper | What you do | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Reading: two compulsory questions on unseen texts | 50 | 2 h 15 |
| **2** | Writing: one compulsory question, then one from a choice | 50 | 2 h |

Paper 1 rewards analysis of how texts work for their audience and purpose. Paper 2 rewards controlled writing in a set form, and a commentary that explains your own choices. Technique: [exam technique](exam-technique.md).

## How to study English Language at AS

- **Read widely in the forms you'll meet:** articles, speeches, reviews, blogs, letters, narratives.
- **For each text, name its audience, purpose and form,** then find the language and structure that serve them.
- **Write, then comment:** after each piece, a few lines on why you made your main choices.
