## 1. Paper 1 Reading

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**1.1 Directed response** ([notes](notes/p1-reading.md))
- Writing a new text for a given audience, purpose and form, using material from a source text.

**1.2 Analysis and comparison** ([notes](notes/p1-reading.md))
- Analysing how a writer's choices of language, structure and form create meaning and effect for an audience and purpose.
- Comparing two texts.

## 2. Paper 2 Writing

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**2.1 Shorter writing and reflective commentary** ([notes](notes/p2-writing.md))
- A short piece in a given form, then a commentary on the language choices you made and why.

**2.2 Extended writing** ([notes](notes/p2-writing.md))
- One longer piece: imaginative or descriptive, or discursive or argumentative, in a given form for a given audience.
