## The year at a glance

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Nov 2026 – Jan 2027 | Pre-Q exams, then a break. Optional: read [README.md](README.md), skim each syllabus page, and find out which English and which Maths second paper you'll take. |
| Term 1 (Feb–Apr) | Write up each topic **within a week of it being taught**, and start its deck. A short weekly review of every written topic. |
| Term 2 (May–Jul) | Same rhythm. Past-paper questions on each topic as soon as it's written; put what the examiner reports say into "This loses marks". |
| Term 3 (Jul–Sep) | Finish new content by about August. Full timed papers, marked against the mark schemes; log every lost mark by type and redo the question two days later. |
| Late Sep – Nov | Exams. Plan by each paper's date: two days out, no new material; the day before, a sweep of that paper's topics. |

## Priority across subjects

- **Every subject gets each topic written up the week it's taught.** Falling behind is what makes AS hard, more than any single topic.
- **When time is short, protect the heaviest paper:** Economics Paper 2 (67%), Business Paper 2 (60%), Maths Paper 1 (60%).
- **Economics and Geography were the hardest at Pre-Q,** so give them the first slot in a busy week.

## How to study

The Pre-Q methods carry over unchanged ([STUDY-PLAN.md](../STUDY-PLAN.md#how-to-study-each-kind-of-content)): Anki for facts, blurting for explanations, drawing diagrams from memory, past papers for methods. AS adds:

| Content | Method |
|---|---|
| Essays (Economics, Business, Geography, English) | Plan many, write a few: a five-line plan for every past question, and one full timed answer per question type each term. Mark against the levels. |
| Data response and unseen resources | Practise on past papers' data: describe it with figures first, then apply the theory to it. |
| Set texts (English Literature) | Key quotations and passages in Anki; for each theme, the passages that show it; timed passage-based answers. |
| Maths and statistics | Past-paper questions on a topic as soon as it's written up; redo every lost mark two days later. |

## Anki

- **One deck tree,** `Cambridge::<Subject>::<topic>`, and one preset per subject with the Pre-Q settings (FSRS on, desired retention 0.90).
- **Two new note types, made once:** **Cambridge Basic** and **Cambridge Cloze**, cloned from the Pre-Q types and renamed ([why](README.md#conventions)).
- **Pre-Q decks** keep running after the exams, as the Pre-Q plan says; suspend Physics.
