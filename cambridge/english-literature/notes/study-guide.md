# English Literature study guide (AS, Oct/Nov 2027)

What to study first and how, through the AS year. The papers, the 2027 set-text options and every topic are on the [syllabus page](../SYLLABUS.md).

## The exam at a glance

| Paper | What you do | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | one drama question and one poetry question, each passage-based or an essay | *(to verify)* |
| **2** | one prose question, then a critical response to an unseen text | *(to verify)* |

Every answer is marked on knowledge of the text, analysis of how it works (language, form, structure), and a clear argument that answers the question. Technique: [exam technique](exam-technique.md).

## How to study literature at AS

- **Chunks, not whole essays:** for each theme, the passages and quotations that show it, and the point each makes.
- **Quotations word for word,** with the speaker and the scene or chapter.
- **Practise the question types,** passage-based and essay, on every text.
