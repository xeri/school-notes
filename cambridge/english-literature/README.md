## Exam technique

- **Split the time evenly** between each paper's two answers, with about 10 minutes of planning for each.
- **Passage-based questions:**
  - Read the passage twice and place it in the whole text: where it comes, and what changes in it.
  - Analyse it closely, then link it to the rest of the text.
- **Essays:**
  - Answer the question in the first paragraph: an argument, not a summary.
  - Each paragraph makes one point, gives a short quotation, analyses its effect, and links back to the question.
  - Use context only where it explains the text.
- **The unseen:**
  1. A first reading for what it's about and its mood.
  2. A second for form, structure and language, noting three or four features that create the effect.
  3. Organise the answer around ideas, not features.
- **Marks:** knowledge of the text, analysis of language, form and structure, a personal response that answers the question, and context *(to verify)*. Answers are marked in levels.

**This loses marks:**
- retelling the plot instead of answering the question;
- naming techniques without their effect;
- long quotations with little comment.

## How to study it

- Learn chunks, not whole essays: for each theme, the passages and quotations that show it, and the point each makes.
- Learn quotations word for word, with the speaker and the scene or chapter.
- From Term 2, do one timed unseen a fortnight.
