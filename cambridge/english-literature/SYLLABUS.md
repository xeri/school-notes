## 1. Paper 1 Drama and Poetry

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**1.1 Drama set text** ([notes](notes/p1-drama.md))
- Close knowledge of the play: plot, characters, themes and structure.
- How the playwright's choices of language, form and staging shape meaning and effect.
- Answering on a passage in relation to the whole play, or writing an essay on the play.

**1.2 Poetry set text** ([notes](notes/p1-poetry.md))
- Close knowledge of the selected poems and the links between them.
- How form, structure and language create meaning and effect.
- Answering on a single poem, or writing an essay that ranges across the selection.

## 2. Paper 2 Prose and Unseen

*Outline, to verify against the syllabus.*

**2.1 Prose set text** ([notes](notes/p2-prose.md))
- Close knowledge of the text: narrative voice, structure, characters and themes, and their context.
- Answering on a passage in relation to the whole text, or writing an essay on it.

**2.2 Unseen poetry, prose or drama** ([notes](notes/p2-unseen.md))
- A critical response to an unseen text: what it's about, and how its form, structure and language shape its effect.
- A clear personal response, supported by close reference to the text.
