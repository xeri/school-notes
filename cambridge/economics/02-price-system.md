# The price system and the microeconomy

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 2, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **2.1 Demand and supply curves:** Individual and market demand and supply; movements along a curve versus shifts; the causes of shifts.
- **2.2 Price elasticity, income elasticity and cross elasticity of demand:** Each formula, its calculation and interpretation; the determinants; PED and total revenue; uses for firms and the government.
- **2.3 Price elasticity of supply:** Formula and interpretation; the determinants (time, spare capacity, stocks).
- **2.4 The interaction of demand and supply:** Equilibrium and disequilibrium; the effects of shifts; what the price mechanism does; joint, competitive and derived demand, and joint supply.
- **2.5 Consumer and producer surplus:** Their meaning and how to show them on a diagram; what changes them; how elasticity affects the size of the change.
