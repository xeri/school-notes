# Economics study guide (AS, Oct/Nov 2027)

What to study first and how, through the AS year. The papers and every topic are on the [syllabus page](../SYLLABUS.md).

## The exam at a glance

| Paper | What you do | Marks | Time | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 30 multiple-choice questions | 30 | 1 hour | 2 minutes a question |
| **2** | A: data response (compulsory). B: one micro essay. C: one macro essay | 60 | 2 hours | 2 minutes a mark |

Paper 2 is two-thirds of the AS. Its marks go mostly to **analysis** (complete cause-and-effect chains, with diagrams) and **evaluation** (a judgement on what the outcome depends on). Paper 1 rewards precise distinctions made fast. Technique for both: [exam technique](exam-technique.md).

## How to study economics at AS

- **The Pre-Q loop still works:** read one chain, close the page, blurt it as numbered steps, draw its diagram, check, then fix the gaps.
- **Add a judgement to every chain:** "this works if…, but…". Evaluation is where most AS marks are.
- **Diagrams are mechanical marks.** Work through [DIAGRAMS.md](../DIAGRAMS.md) until each one is automatic.
- **From Term 2,** plan five past-paper essays for every one you write in full, and do ten multiple-choice questions a day on written topics.
