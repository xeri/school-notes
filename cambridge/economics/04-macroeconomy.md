# The macroeconomy

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 4, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **4.1 National income statistics:** GDP, GNI and NNI; nominal and real; per head; what the figures are used for and their limits.
- **4.2 Introduction to the circular flow of income:** Flows in closed and open economies; injections and withdrawals; how a change affects national income.
- **4.3 Aggregate demand and aggregate supply analysis:** The components of AD and what shifts them; short-run and long-run AS; macroeconomic equilibrium; the effects of shifts on output and the price level.
- **4.4 Economic growth:** Actual and potential growth; how growth is measured; its causes and consequences.
- **4.5 Unemployment:** How unemployment is measured; its types and causes; its consequences.
- **4.6 Price stability:** Inflation, deflation and disinflation; how prices are measured; demand-pull and cost-push causes; consequences.

**From Pre-Q:** [Macroeconomics and policy](../../T4-ECQ/notes/macroeconomics.md). **New at AS:** AD/AS analysis as the main tool; the circular flow; national income statistics.
