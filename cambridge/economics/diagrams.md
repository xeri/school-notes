# Economics diagrams (AS)

Every diagram AS Paper 2 can ask for, as a checklist. Each gets a full entry (axes, curves, what moves and why, and what loses marks) when its topic is written up. The Pre-Q versions are in [the Pre-Q diagram sheet](../../T4-ECQ/DIAGRAMS.md).

## Rules for every diagram

- Label both axes (Price and Quantity, or Price level and Real GDP) and every curve.
- Show the original and the new equilibrium, with dotted lines to both axes.
- Draw an arrow for every shift, and refer to the diagram in your writing.
