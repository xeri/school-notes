# Government macroeconomic intervention

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 5, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **5.1 Government macroeconomic policy objectives:** Growth, low unemployment, stable prices and a sustainable current account.
- **5.2 Fiscal policy:** The budget (deficit and surplus); types of tax; government spending; expansionary and contractionary policy and its effect on AD.
- **5.3 Monetary policy:** Interest rates and the money supply; expansionary and contractionary policy; the transmission to AD.
- **5.4 Supply-side policy:** Market-based and interventionist policies (education and training, deregulation, privatisation, incentives) and their effect on long-run AS.
