# Basic economic ideas and resource allocation

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 1, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **1.1 Scarcity, choice and opportunity cost:** The basic economic problem: unlimited wants, limited resources, so choices and an opportunity cost.
- **1.2 Economic methodology:** Economics as a social science; positive and normative statements; ceteris paribus; the short run, long run and very long run.
- **1.3 Factors of production:** Land, labour, capital and enterprise, and their rewards; specialisation and the division of labour; the role of the entrepreneur.
- **1.4 Resource allocation in different economic systems:** What, how and for whom to produce; market, planned and mixed economies, and how each answers those questions.
- **1.5 Production possibility curves:** Points inside, on and outside the curve; constant and increasing opportunity cost; what shifts the curve.
- **1.6 Classification of goods and services:** Free and economic goods; private and public goods; merit and demerit goods, and information failure.
