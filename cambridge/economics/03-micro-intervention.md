# Government microeconomic intervention

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 3, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **3.1 Reasons for government intervention in markets:** Why markets can give the wrong outcome: merit and demerit goods, public goods, information failure.
- **3.2 Methods and effects of government intervention in markets:** Indirect taxes and subsidies and their incidence; maximum and minimum prices; direct provision; regulation; information; unintended consequences.
- **3.3 Addressing income and wealth inequality:** Equity and equality; income and wealth; the policies that redistribute them and their effects.
