# International economic issues

*Status: skeleton (AS topic 6, Papers 1 and 2). Write it up when it's taught, then delete this line.*

**What the syllabus asks** *(outline, to verify)*:

- **6.1 The reasons for international trade:** Specialisation; absolute and comparative advantage; the terms of trade; the benefits and costs of trade.
- **6.2 Protectionism:** Tariffs, quotas, subsidies, embargoes and other barriers; the arguments for and against.
- **6.3 Current account of the balance of payments:** Its components; the causes and consequences of deficits and surpluses.
- **6.4 Exchange rates:** Floating, fixed and managed systems; what determines a floating rate; appreciation and depreciation; the effects on the economy.
- **6.5 Policies to correct imbalances in the current account of the balance of payments:** How fiscal, monetary, supply-side and protectionist policies affect the current account.
