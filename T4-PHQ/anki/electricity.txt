Pre-Q Basic	Magnetic domains?	Tiny regions acting as magnets; aligned when magnetised	
Pre-Q Basic	Magnetic field?	Region where a magnetic pole feels a force	
Pre-Q Basic	Magnetic field direction at a point?	Direction of force on a N pole	
Pre-Q Basic	Induced magnetism?	Magnetic material near a magnet becomes a magnet itself	
Pre-Q Basic	Soft magnetic material?	Loses magnetism easily (soft iron: cores)	Opposite of hard
Pre-Q Basic	Hard magnetic material?	Keeps magnetism (steel: permanent magnets)	Opposite of soft
Pre-Q Basic	Neutral point (magnetism)?	Point where two fields cancel	
Pre-Q Basic	Electric field?	Region where a charge feels a force	
Pre-Q Basic	Electric field direction?	Force on a positive charge	
Pre-Q Basic	Current?	Rate of flow of charge	
Pre-Q Basic	Direct current (d.c.)?	Current flowing in one direction only	
Pre-Q Basic	Alternating current (a.c.)?	Current that repeatedly reverses direction	
Pre-Q Basic	Conventional current?	Flow from + to −, opposite to electron flow	
Pre-Q Basic	E.m.f. (electromotive force)?	Work done by source per unit charge round a circuit	Not a force
Pre-Q Basic	Potential difference (p.d.)?	Work done per unit charge passing through a component	
Pre-Q Basic	Ohm's law?	Metal at constant temperature: I ∝ V	
Pre-Q Basic	Kilowatt-hour (kWh)?	Energy transferred by 1 kW in 1 hour	
Pre-Q Basic	Potential divider?	Two series resistors sharing the supply p.d.	
Pre-Q Basic	Input transducer?	Sensor turning a physical change into an electrical one	
Pre-Q Basic	Relay?	Small coil current switches a separate, larger current	
Pre-Q Basic	Diode?	Lets current through in one direction only	
Pre-Q Basic	Rectifier?	Converts a.c. to d.c. (a diode)	
Pre-Q Basic	Light-emitting diode (LED)?	Diode that gives out light when conducting	
Pre-Q Basic	Fuse?	Thin wire that melts if current exceeds its rating	
Pre-Q Basic	Trip switch (circuit breaker)?	Electromagnet opens a switch on excess current; resettable	
Pre-Q Basic	Earthing?	Low-resistance path from metal case to earth	
Pre-Q Basic	Double insulation?	Non-conducting case, no exposed metal; no earth wire	
Pre-Q Basic	Lenz's law?	Induced e.m.f. opposes the change causing it	
Pre-Q Basic	Motor effect?	Force on a current-carrying wire in a magnetic field	
Pre-Q Basic	Only true test for a magnet?	Repulsion	Attraction works on any magnetic material
Pre-Q Basic	Field line direction outside a magnet?	N to S	
Pre-Q Basic	Field strength shown by?	Spacing of field lines (closer = stronger)
