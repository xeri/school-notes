Pre-Q Basic	Mass?	Measure of inertia and quantity of matter (kg, scalar)	
Pre-Q Basic	Weight?	Gravitational force on a mass (N; vector; acts downwards)	
Pre-Q Basic	Gravitational field strength?	Force per unit mass (N/kg)	
Pre-Q Basic	Resultant force?	Vector sum of all forces on a body	
Pre-Q Basic	Friction?	Force between surfaces that impedes motion and causes heating	
Pre-Q Basic	Air resistance (drag)?	Friction from air opposing motion	
Pre-Q Basic	Newton's first law?	No resultant force: stays at rest or constant velocity	
Pre-Q Cloze	Newton's third law: force pairs are {{c1::equal}} in size, {{c2::opposite}} in direction, of the {{c3::same type}}, and act on {{c4::different objects}}		So they never cancel
Pre-Q Basic	Hooke's law?	Extension ∝ force, up to the limit of proportionality	
Pre-Q Basic	Limit of proportionality?	Point where the force–extension line stops being straight	
Pre-Q Basic	Extension (spring)?	Stretched length minus original length	Not total length
Pre-Q Basic	Spring constant?	Force per unit extension (N/m)	
Pre-Q Basic	Moment of a force?	Force × perpendicular distance from pivot to line of action	
Pre-Q Basic	Equilibrium (forces)?	No resultant force and no resultant moment	2 conditions
Pre-Q Basic	Centre of mass (centre of gravity)?	Point where the whole weight seems to act	
Pre-Q Basic	Stable equilibrium?	Tilted, weight line stays inside base; returns	
Pre-Q Basic	Unstable equilibrium?	Tilted, weight line falls outside base; topples	
Pre-Q Basic	Neutral equilibrium?	Displacement gives no moment either way (ball on table)	
Pre-Q Basic	Momentum?	Mass × velocity (vector)	
Pre-Q Basic	Impulse?	Force × time; equals change in momentum	
Pre-Q Basic	Isolated system?	System with no external forces	
Pre-Q Basic	Conservation of momentum?	Isolated system: total momentum before = total after	
Pre-Q Cloze	Weight: \(W = {{c1::mg}}\), unit {{c2::N}}		g in N/kg
Pre-Q Cloze	Field strength: \(g = {{c1::\dfrac{W}{m} }}\), unit {{c2::N/kg}}		
Pre-Q Basic	g in N/kg is numerically equal to?	Free-fall acceleration in m/s²	
Pre-Q Basic	Mass vs weight on the Moon	Mass unchanged; weight about one sixth	
Pre-Q Basic	Guinea-and-feather experiment shows?	In a vacuum all objects fall with the same acceleration	
Pre-Q Cloze	A force can change a body's {{c1::size or shape}}, {{c2::speed}} or {{c3::direction}}		3 effects
Pre-Q Cloze	Newton's second law: \(F = {{c1::ma}}\)	F is the resultant force	
Pre-Q Cloze	Hooke's law: \(F = {{c1::kx}}\); k in {{c2::N/m}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Spring constant: \(k = {{c1::\dfrac{F}{x} }}\)		Rearrange Hooke's law
Pre-Q Basic	Find k from a force–extension graph?	Gradient of the straight section (force on y-axis)	If axes are swapped, gradient = 1/k
Pre-Q Basic	Direction of the force in circular motion?	Towards the centre, perpendicular to velocity	
Pre-Q Cloze	Circular motion: \(F = {{c1::\dfrac{mv^2}{r} }}\)		
Pre-Q Cloze	Circular motion: larger force, same m and r → {{c1::larger speed}}; larger force, same m and v → {{c2::smaller radius}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Moment: \(M = {{c1::Fd}}\), unit {{c2::N·m}}	d perpendicular to line of action	
Pre-Q Cloze	Principle of moments: in equilibrium, Σ {{c1::clockwise}} moments = Σ {{c2::anticlockwise}} moments about the same {{c3::pivot}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Where does a uniform beam's weight act?	At its centre	
Pre-Q Basic	Find a lamina's centre of mass?	Hang from 3 holes with plumb-line; lines cross	
Pre-Q Basic	What makes an object hard to topple?	Wide base and low centre of mass	
Pre-Q Cloze	Momentum: \(p = {{c1::mv}}\), unit {{c2::kg·m/s}}		Vector: signs matter
Pre-Q Cloze	Impulse: \(Ft = {{c1::mv - mu}}\), unit {{c2::N·s}}		Change in momentum
Pre-Q Cloze	Resultant force: \(F = {{c1::\dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t} }}\)	Rate of change of momentum	
Pre-Q Basic	How do crumple zones and air bags work?	Longer impact time, smaller force	Same momentum change
Pre-Q Basic	Explosion from rest: total momentum after?	Zero
