Pre-Q Basic	Orbit?	Curved path of one body round another under gravity	
Pre-Q Basic	Light-year?	Distance light travels in one year	A distance, not a time
Pre-Q Basic	Galaxy?	Group of many billions of stars	
Pre-Q Basic	Redshift?	Increase in observed wavelength from receding galaxies	
Pre-Q Basic	Hubble constant?	Recession speed ÷ distance of a galaxy	
Pre-Q Basic	Cosmic microwave background radiation (CMBR)?	Microwaves from all directions: early-Universe radiation stretched by expansion	
Pre-Q Basic	Accretion model?	Solar System formed from a collapsing, rotating gas-and-dust disc	
Pre-Q Basic	What causes day and night?	Earth rotating on its axis every ~24 h	
Pre-Q Basic	What causes the seasons?	Earth's tilted axis as it orbits the Sun	Not distance from the Sun
Pre-Q Basic	What causes the Moon's phases?	Seeing different parts of its sunlit half as it orbits	About one month

Pre-Q Basic	Where is the asteroid belt?	Between Mars and Jupiter	
Pre-Q Basic	Planetary orbit shape?	Ellipse, Sun at one focus (not centre)	
Pre-Q Basic	Force keeping a planet in orbit?	Gravitational attraction of the Sun

Pre-Q Basic	Planets further from the Sun: orbital speed and period?	Slower speed, longer period
