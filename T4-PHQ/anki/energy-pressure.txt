#deck:Physics::4 Density, energy and pressure
Density formula	\(\rho = m/V\)
Density of water	1.00 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³
Condition for an object to float	Its density is less than the fluid's
Volume of a sphere	\(\tfrac43\pi r^3\)
Volume of a triangular prism	\(\tfrac12 bh \times\) length
How do you find the volume of an irregular solid?	Displacement — note the water level rise in a measuring cylinder, or collect the overflow from a eureka can
How do you find the density of a liquid?	Weigh an empty measuring cylinder, add a measured volume, weigh again; mass = difference
Most common error in a density calculation	Not converting the volume to m³ before dividing
Name the six energy stores in scope	Kinetic, gravitational potential, chemical, elastic (strain), nuclear, internal (thermal)
Four ways energy is transferred	Forces (mechanical working), electric currents (electrical working), heating, waves
Kinetic energy formula	\(E_k = \tfrac12 mv^2\)
Gravitational potential energy formula	\(\Delta E_p = mg\Delta h\)
State conservation of energy	The total energy of a closed system is constant — energy is never destroyed
What is dissipation?	Energy spreading out among objects and the surroundings as thermal energy, where it is no longer useful
What does arrow width represent on a Sankey diagram?	The amount of energy — widths must add up
Work done formula	\(W = Fd = \Delta E\)
In which direction must the distance be measured for work done?	In the direction of the force
How much work does a perpendicular force do?	None — it changes direction but not speed
Power formula	\(P = \Delta E / t\)
Efficiency formula (energy)	useful energy output / total energy input × 100%
Efficiency formula (power)	useful power output / total power input × 100%
Can efficiency exceed 100%?	No
Which energy resources do NOT originate from the Sun?	Geothermal, nuclear and tidal
How does the Sun release energy?	Nuclear fusion of small nuclei
Advantages of wind power	Renewable, no fuel cost, no CO₂ in operation
Disadvantages of wind power	Intermittent, low output per turbine, visual and noise impact
Advantages of nuclear fission	Huge output, no CO₂ in operation, reliable
Disadvantages of nuclear fission	Radioactive waste, high build and decommissioning cost, non-renewable fuel
Five criteria for judging an energy resource	Renewability, cost, reliability, scale, environmental impact
Pressure formula	\(P = F/A\)
Must the force be parallel or normal to the surface?	Normal (perpendicular)
Why do snowshoes stop you sinking?	The same weight spread over a larger area gives a smaller pressure
Liquid pressure formula	\(P = \rho g h\)
What is h in P = ρgh?	The depth below the liquid surface — never the volume or width
How does liquid pressure vary with direction at a given depth?	It acts equally in all directions
How does a mercury barometer work?	An inverted mercury-filled tube stands in a reservoir; the column height at which it balances atmospheric pressure measures that pressure, with a vacuum above
Typical atmospheric pressure in mm of mercury	About 760 mm
How does a manometer measure pressure?	The difference in liquid levels h in a U-tube gives the excess pressure as ρgh
