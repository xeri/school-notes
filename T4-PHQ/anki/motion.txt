Definition of speed	The rate of change of distance
Average speed formula	average speed = total distance / total time
Difference between speed and velocity	Speed is a scalar; velocity is speed in a stated direction (a vector)
Definition of acceleration	The rate of change of velocity, \(a=(v-u)/t\)
What is deceleration?	Negative acceleration — the speed decreases
What does the gradient of a distance–time graph give?	Speed
What does the gradient of a speed–time graph give?	Acceleration
What does the area under a speed–time graph give?	Distance travelled
Distance–time graph: horizontal line means?	At rest
Distance–time graph: straight slope means?	Constant speed
Distance–time graph: curve means?	Changing speed
Speed–time graph: horizontal line means?	Constant speed, zero acceleration
Speed–time graph: straight slope means?	Constant acceleration
Speed–time graph: curve means?	Changing acceleration
Value of free-fall acceleration near Earth	\(g = 9.81\) m/s²
What is terminal velocity?	The steady speed reached when drag grows equal in magnitude to weight, so the resultant force is zero
Why does a falling body's acceleration decrease?	Drag increases with speed, so the resultant force (weight − drag) falls
Shape of the speed–time graph for a body falling in air	A curve of decreasing gradient flattening to a horizontal line
Why do a coin and a feather fall together in a vacuum?	No drag acts, so both have acceleration g regardless of mass
What does s stand for in SUVAT?	Displacement (m)
What does u stand for in SUVAT?	Initial velocity (m/s)
What does v stand for in SUVAT?	Final velocity (m/s)
What does a stand for in SUVAT?	Constant acceleration (m/s²)
SUVAT equation linking v, u, a, t	\(v = u + at\)
SUVAT equation linking s, u, a, t	\(s = ut + \tfrac12 at^2\)
SUVAT equation linking s, u, v, t	\(s = \tfrac12(u+v)t\)
SUVAT equation with no t	\(v^2 = u^2 + 2as\)
When does SUVAT reduce to s = ut?	When the acceleration is zero
What condition must hold for SUVAT to apply?	The acceleration must be constant (or zero)
Graphical meaning of \(s = ut + \tfrac12at^2\)	The area under a velocity–time graph (rectangle + triangle)
Graphical meaning of a in \(v = u + at\)	The gradient of the velocity–time graph
Object starts at 2.0 m/s, accelerates 3.0 m/s² for 4.0 s: final velocity?	\(v = 2.0 + 3.0(4.0) = 14\) m/s
Object starts at 2.0 m/s, accelerates 3.0 m/s² for 4.0 s: distance?	\(s = 2.0(4.0) + \tfrac12(3.0)(16) = 32\) m
Stone dropped from 45 m: speed on landing?	\(v^2 = 2(9.81)(45) \Rightarrow v = 30\) m/s
How do you choose which SUVAT equation to use?	Pick the one that omits the quantity you were not given and do not need
What must you do before using negative velocities?	Choose and state a positive direction, then keep it
