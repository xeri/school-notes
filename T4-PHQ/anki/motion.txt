Pre-Q Basic	Speed?	Distance travelled per unit time (scalar)	
Pre-Q Basic	Velocity?	Speed in a stated direction (vector)	
Pre-Q Basic	Acceleration?	Change in velocity per unit time	
Pre-Q Basic	Deceleration?	Negative acceleration: speed decreasing	
Pre-Q Basic	Terminal velocity?	Steady speed when drag equals weight	Resultant force?

Pre-Q Cloze	Acceleration: \(a = {{c1::\dfrac{v-u}{t} }}\), unit {{c2::m/s²}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Constant speed but changing velocity: example?	Moving in a circle at steady speed	
Pre-Q Cloze	Distance–time graph: gradient = {{c1::speed}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Speed–time graph: gradient = {{c1::acceleration}}; area under line = {{c2::distance travelled}}

Pre-Q Cloze	Speed–time graph: horizontal = {{c1::constant speed}}; straight slope = {{c2::constant acceleration}}; curve = {{c3::changing acceleration}}; sloping down = {{c4::deceleration}}		4 shapes
Pre-Q Basic	Speed at an instant on a curved distance–time graph?	Gradient of the tangent at that point	
Pre-Q Basic	Why use a large triangle for a gradient?	Reduces reading error	
Pre-Q Basic	Area under a sloping speed–time section?	Triangle: ½ × base × height	Not a rectangle
Pre-Q Cloze	Free-fall acceleration near Earth: \(g = {{c1::9.81}}\) {{c2::m/s²}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Falling in air: at release acceleration = {{c1::g}}; drag {{c2::rises}} with speed; at terminal velocity resultant = {{c3::zero}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Why does a falling object's acceleration decrease?	Drag rises with speed, so resultant force falls	
Pre-Q Basic	Speed–time graph for a fall through air?	Curve of falling gradient, flattening to horizontal	
Pre-Q Basic	Speed–time graph for a fall in a vacuum?	Straight line of gradient g	No drag
Pre-Q Basic	Forces on a body at terminal velocity?	Weight and drag, equal and opposite	Not "no forces"
Pre-Q Basic	Skydiver opens parachute: effect?	Drag &gt; weight: decelerates to a lower terminal velocity	Which way is the resultant?
Pre-Q Basic	Terminal velocity: liquid vs air	Liquid: lower, reached sooner
