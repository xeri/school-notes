Pre-Q Basic	What do waves transfer?	Energy, not matter	
Pre-Q Basic	Wavelength?	Distance between matching points on consecutive waves	
Pre-Q Basic	Frequency?	Oscillations per second (Hz)	
Pre-Q Basic	Period (wave)?	Time for one oscillation (s)	
Pre-Q Basic	Amplitude?	Maximum displacement from the rest position	Not crest to trough
Pre-Q Basic	Wave speed?	Distance the wave pattern travels per second	
Pre-Q Basic	Wavefront?	Line joining points oscillating in step	
Pre-Q Basic	Transverse wave?	Vibration at right angles to direction of travel	
Pre-Q Basic	Longitudinal wave?	Vibration parallel to direction of travel	
Pre-Q Basic	Diffraction?	Waves spreading out after passing a gap or edge	
Pre-Q Basic	Interference (superposition)?	Overlapping waves' displacements add	Constructive vs destructive
Pre-Q Basic	Law of reflection?	Angle of incidence = angle of reflection	Measured from the normal
Pre-Q Basic	Refractive index?	Speed of light in vacuum ÷ speed in material	
Pre-Q Basic	Critical angle?	Angle of incidence giving 90° refraction	In the denser medium
Pre-Q Basic	Total internal reflection?	All light reflected inside the denser medium	
Pre-Q Basic	Principal focus?	Point where parallel rays meet after a converging lens	
Pre-Q Basic	Focal length?	Distance from lens centre to principal focus	
Pre-Q Basic	Principal axis?	Line through the lens centre, at 90° to the lens	
Pre-Q Basic	Real image?	Formed where rays actually cross; can be on a screen	
Pre-Q Basic	Virtual image?	Where rays only appear to come from; not on a screen	
Pre-Q Basic	Dispersion?	Splitting white light into its colours	Prism
Pre-Q Basic	Monochromatic light?	Light of a single frequency	
Pre-Q Basic	Echo?	Reflection of sound from a hard surface	
Pre-Q Basic	Ultrasound?	Sound above 20 kHz (beyond human hearing)
