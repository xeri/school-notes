Pre-Q Basic	Dependent variable?	The variable you measure

Pre-Q Basic	Random error?	Scatter either side; reduce by repeating and averaging	Not systematic
Pre-Q Basic	Systematic error?	All readings shifted one way; fix by calibration or zero check	Not random
Pre-Q Basic	Zero error?	Instrument reads non-zero with nothing loaded; subtract it	
Pre-Q Basic	Anomalous result: correct response?	Check and repeat; exclude only with a stated reason	
Pre-Q Basic	Why time 20 oscillations, not one?	Reaction-time error becomes a tiny percentage	
Pre-Q Basic	Justify an instrument choice by?	The precision needed for the size of the quantity
