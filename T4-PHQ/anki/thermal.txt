Pre-Q Basic	Temperature (molecular)?	Measure of average molecular kinetic energy	
Pre-Q Basic	Internal energy?	Total molecular kinetic + potential energy	
Pre-Q Basic	Heat?	Energy transferred from hotter to colder body	
Pre-Q Basic	Absolute zero?	0 K = −273 °C; molecular motion stops	
Pre-Q Basic	Brownian motion?	Random jitter of visible particles hit unevenly by molecules	
Pre-Q Basic	Thermal capacity?	Energy to raise a whole object by 1 K	Whole object, not per kg
Pre-Q Basic	Specific heat capacity?	Energy to raise 1 kg by 1 K	Per kg
Pre-Q Basic	Specific latent heat?	Energy per kg to change state at constant temperature	
Pre-Q Basic	Melting point?	Fixed temperature at which a pure solid melts	
Pre-Q Basic	Boiling point?	Fixed temperature at which a liquid boils throughout	
Pre-Q Basic	Evaporation?	Escape of the fastest molecules from a liquid's surface	
Pre-Q Basic	Fixed points (thermometer)?	Reference temperatures defining a scale: 0 °C and 100 °C	
Pre-Q Basic	Ideal gas?	Gas that obeys the gas laws exactly	
Pre-Q Basic	Conduction?	Energy passed through matter with no bulk movement	
Pre-Q Basic	Convection?	Thermal transfer by bulk movement of a fluid	
Pre-Q Basic	Thermal radiation?	Energy carried by infrared waves; needs no medium	
Pre-Q Cloze	Solid: {{c1::regular lattice, touching}}; particles {{c2::vibrate about fixed positions}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Liquid: {{c1::close but disordered}}; particles {{c2::slide past each other}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Gas: {{c1::far apart}}; particles move {{c2::fast and randomly}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Kelvin: \(T/\mathrm{K} = {{c1::\theta/^\circ\mathrm{C} + 273}}\)	A change of 1 °C = a change of 1 K	
Pre-Q Basic	Gas pressure: molecular explanation?	Molecules hit walls, change momentum: force over area	Think Δp per collision
Pre-Q Cloze	Boyle's law: \(pV = {{c1::\text{constant} }}\) at constant {{c2::temperature}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Pressure law: \(\dfrac{p}{T} = {{c1::\text{constant} }}\) at constant {{c2::volume}}		T in K
Pre-Q Cloze	Charles's law: \(\dfrac{V}{T} = {{c1::\text{constant} }}\) at constant {{c2::pressure}}		T in K
Pre-Q Cloze	Combined gas law: \(\dfrac{p_1V_1}{T_1} = {{c1::\dfrac{p_2V_2}{T_2} }}\)	Fixed mass of gas	T in K
Pre-Q Basic	p–V graph at constant temperature?	Curve (inverse proportion); p vs 1/V is straight	
Pre-Q Basic	When is the combined gas law unreliable?	Very high pressure, or near liquefying	Real gas, not ideal
Pre-Q Basic	Smaller gas volume: why more pressure?	Molecules hit walls more often	
Pre-Q Basic	Hotter gas at constant volume: why more pressure?	Faster molecules hit harder and more often	
Pre-Q Basic	Why does evaporation cool a liquid?	Average KE of remaining molecules drops	Which molecules leave?
Pre-Q Cloze	Faster evaporation: {{c1::higher temperature}}, {{c2::larger surface area}}, {{c3::draught over surface}}		3 factors
Pre-Q Basic	Evaporation vs boiling	Evaporation: surface only, any temp. Boiling: throughout at fixed temp	
Pre-Q Basic	Thermal expansion order?	Gases ≫ liquids &gt; solids	Weaker forces, more expansion
Pre-Q Basic	Liquid-in-glass thermometer works how?	Liquid expands more than glass; rises up capillary	
Pre-Q Cloze	Thermometer: larger bulb → {{c1::more sensitive}}; narrower capillary → more sensitive but {{c2::smaller range}}; uniform bore → {{c3::linear scale}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Thermocouple?	Two-metal junction e.m.f. sensor for high, rapidly varying temperatures	Tiny junction
