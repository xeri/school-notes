#deck:Physics::1 Measurement, units and vectors
How many SI base quantities are there?	7
SI base unit of length	metre (m)
SI base unit of mass	kilogram (kg)
SI base unit of time	second (s)
SI base unit of electric current	ampere (A)
SI base unit of thermodynamic temperature	kelvin (K)
SI base unit of amount of substance	mole (mol)
SI base unit of luminous intensity	candela (cd)
Unit of force	newton (N) = kg·m/s²
Unit of pressure	pascal (Pa) = N/m²
Unit of energy	joule (J) = N·m
Unit of power	watt (W) = J/s
Unit of potential difference	volt (V) = J/C
Unit of charge	coulomb (C) = A·s
Unit of momentum	kg·m/s
Convert 72 km/h to m/s	72 × 1000 / 3600 = 20 m/s
Convert 1 cm³ to m³	\(10^{-6}\) m³ (all three dimensions ÷ 100)
Convert 2.7 g/cm³ to kg/m³	2700 kg/m³
Speed of light in standard form	\(3.00\times10^8\) m/s
Precision of a vernier calliper	±0.1 mm
Precision of a micrometer screw gauge	±0.01 mm
Precision of a metre rule	±1 mm
How is a voltmeter connected?	In parallel across the component
How is an ammeter connected?	In series with the component
Formula for an average of n readings	average = (sum of n readings) / n
Why time 20 oscillations instead of 1?	The same reaction error (~±0.2 s) becomes a much smaller percentage of a longer interval
What error do repeats reduce?	Random error (they do nothing for systematic error)
What is a normal?	A construction line drawn at 90° to a surface, used as the reference for angles
How do you avoid parallax error?	View the scale with your eye directly in line with the mark
Definition of a scalar	A quantity with magnitude only
Definition of a vector	A quantity with magnitude and direction
Three scalar examples	Distance, speed, mass (also time, energy, temperature)
Three vector examples	Displacement, velocity, weight (also force, acceleration, momentum)
Walk 3 m east then 4 m north: distance and displacement?	Distance 7 m; displacement 5 m
How do you find a resultant by scale diagram?	Draw vectors head-to-tail to a stated scale; the resultant runs from the first tail to the last head
Resultant of 3.0 N east and 4.0 N north	5.0 N at 37° east of north
How should axes on a graph be labelled?	With the quantity AND its unit
How do you find a gradient accurately?	Use a large triangle on the best-fit line, Δy ÷ Δx, and state the units
What are the units of a distance–time gradient?	m/s — it is a speed
What are the units of a speed–time gradient?	m/s² — it is an acceleration
