# Physics formulas

**No formula sheet is provided in the exam.** Every equation below must be recalled. It covers every equation in the Pre-Q objectives and in the Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 Extended syllabus (rows marked † are IGCSE-only additions not in the school list).

## Measurement

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Average | $\bar{x} = \dfrac{\sum x}{n}$ | $n$ readings |
| Gradient | $\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$ | units of $y$ ÷ units of $x$ |
| Resultant of perpendicular vectors | $R = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$, angle $= \tan^{-1}(a/b)$ | same units as $a$, $b$ |

Prefixes: G $10^9$, M $10^6$, k $10^3$, c $10^{-2}$, m $10^{-3}$, μ $10^{-6}$, n $10^{-9}$.

## Motion

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | $v = \dfrac{s}{t}$ | $v$ m/s, $s$ distance m, $t$ s |
| Average speed | $\dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$ | m/s |
| Acceleration | $a = \dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \dfrac{v-u}{t}$ | $a$ m/s², $u$, $v$ m/s, $t$ s |
| SUVAT | $v = u + at$ | $s$ displacement m |
| | $s = ut + \tfrac12 at^2$ | |
| | $s = \tfrac12(u+v)t$ | |
| | $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ | |
| Distance from a speed–time graph | area under the line | m |
| Acceleration from a speed–time graph | gradient | m/s² |

Free fall: $g = 9.81$ m/s² (IGCSE uses 9.8).

## Forces and momentum

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | $W = mg$ | $W$ N, $m$ kg, $g$ N/kg |
| Gravitational field strength † | $g = \dfrac{W}{m}$ | N/kg (= m/s²) |
| Newton's second law | $F = ma$ | $F$ N (resultant), $a$ m/s² |
| Hooke's law | $F = kx$ | $k$ N/m, $x$ extension m |
| Spring constant † | $k = \dfrac{F}{x}$ | force per unit extension, N/m |
| Circular motion (Pre-Q only) | $F = \dfrac{mv^2}{r}$ | $r$ m |
| Moment | $M = Fd$ | N·m; $d$ = perpendicular distance from pivot to line of action, m |
| Principle of moments | $\sum M_{cw} = \sum M_{acw}$ | about the same pivot |
| Momentum | $p = mv$ | kg·m/s |
| Impulse | $Ft = \Delta(mv) = mv - mu$ | N·s (= kg·m/s) |
| Resultant force | $F = \dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$ | N |
| Conservation of momentum | total $p$ before = total $p$ after | isolated system |

## Density, energy, pressure

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Density | $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V}$ | kg/m³ or g/cm³; 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³ |
| Work done | $W = Fd = \Delta E$ | J; $d$ in the direction of the force |
| Kinetic energy | $E_k = \tfrac12 mv^2$ | J |
| Change in gravitational potential energy | $\Delta E_p = mg\Delta h$ | J |
| Power | $P = \dfrac{W}{t} = \dfrac{\Delta E}{t}$ | W = J/s |
| Efficiency (energy) | $\dfrac{\text{useful energy output}}{\text{total energy input}}\times100\%$ | no unit |
| Efficiency (power) | $\dfrac{\text{useful power output}}{\text{total power input}}\times100\%$ | no unit |
| Pressure | $p = \dfrac{F}{A}$ | Pa = N/m²; $F$ normal to the surface |
| Liquid pressure | $\Delta p = \rho g \Delta h$ | Pa; $\Delta h$ depth m |

## Thermal

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Kelvin conversion | $T/\mathrm{K} = \theta/^\circ\mathrm{C} + 273$ | absolute zero = 0 K = −273 °C |
| Thermal capacity | $C = mc$ | J/K |
| Specific heat capacity | $Q = mc\Delta T$, $c = \dfrac{\Delta E}{m\Delta\theta}$ | $c$ J/(kg·K) = J/(kg·°C) |
| Specific latent heat (Pre-Q) | $Q = ml$ | $l$ J/kg |
| Heat exchange | energy lost by hot body = energy gained by cold body | J |
| Electrical heating (experiments) | $Q = VIt$ | J |
| Boyle's law | $pV = \text{constant}$, $p_1V_1 = p_2V_2$ | fixed mass, constant $T$ |
| Pressure law (Gay-Lussac) | $\dfrac{p}{T} = \text{constant}$ | constant $V$, $T$ in K |
| Charles's law | $\dfrac{V}{T} = \text{constant}$ | constant $p$, $T$ in K |
| Combined gas law | $\dfrac{p_1V_1}{T_1} = \dfrac{p_2V_2}{T_2}$ | $T$ in K |

Specific heat capacity of water: about 4200 J/(kg·K). Water melts at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C.

## Waves

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Wave equation | $v = f\lambda$ | $v$ m/s, $f$ Hz, $\lambda$ m |
| Period | $T = \dfrac{1}{f}$ | s |
| Law of reflection | $i = r$ | angles from the normal |
| Refractive index | $n = \dfrac{\sin i}{\sin r}$ | no unit; $i$ in air |
| | $n = \dfrac{\text{speed in vacuum}}{\text{speed in medium}} = \dfrac{c}{v}$ | |
| Critical angle | $n = \dfrac{1}{\sin c}$ | |
| Echo (speed of sound) | $v = \dfrac{2d}{t}$ | $d$ distance to reflector |
| Sonar / ultrasound depth † | $d = \dfrac{vt}{2}$ | $t$ there-and-back time |

Speed of EM waves in a vacuum: $c = 3.0\times10^8$ m/s. Speed of sound in air: 330–350 m/s. Audible range: 20 Hz – 20 000 Hz; ultrasound > 20 kHz.

## Electricity and magnetism

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Current | $I = \dfrac{Q}{t}$ | $I$ A, $Q$ C, $t$ s |
| e.m.f. † | $E = \dfrac{W}{Q}$ | V = J/C |
| Potential difference † | $V = \dfrac{W}{Q}$ | V = J/C |
| Resistance | $R = \dfrac{V}{I}$ | Ω |
| Resistivity (Pre-Q) | $R = \dfrac{\rho L}{A}$ | $R \propto L$, $R \propto 1/A$ |
| Series resistance | $R_T = R_1 + R_2 + \dots$ | Ω |
| Parallel resistance | $\dfrac{1}{R_T} = \dfrac{1}{R_1} + \dfrac{1}{R_2} + \dots$; for two, $R_T = \dfrac{R_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}$ | Ω |
| Junction rule † | $\sum I_{in} = \sum I_{out}$ | A |
| Series p.d.s | $V_{supply} = V_1 + V_2 + \dots$ | V |
| Electrical power | $P = IV$ | W |
| Electrical energy | $E = IVt$ | J |
| Energy in kWh † | $E\,(\text{kWh}) = P\,(\text{kW}) \times t\,(\text{h})$ | 1 kWh = $3.6\times10^6$ J |
| Cost † | cost = kWh × price per kWh | |
| Potential divider | $\dfrac{V_1}{V_2} = \dfrac{R_1}{R_2}$, $V_{out} = V_{in}\dfrac{R_2}{R_1+R_2}$ | V |
| Cable loss | $P = I^2R$ | W |
| Transformer turns | $\dfrac{V_p}{V_s} = \dfrac{N_p}{N_s}$ | p primary, s secondary |
| Transformer power (100% efficient) | $I_pV_p = I_sV_s$ | W |

1 V = 1 J/C. Fleming's **left** hand = motor (force); **right** hand = generator (induced current).

## Atomic physics and space

| Quantity | Equation | Symbols and units |
|---|---|---|
| Nuclide notation | ${}^{A}_{Z}\mathrm{X}$, neutrons $= A - Z$ | $A$ nucleon number, $Z$ proton number |
| α-decay | ${}^{A}_{Z}\mathrm{X} \rightarrow {}^{A-4}_{Z-2}\mathrm{Y} + {}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He}$ | $A - 4$, $Z - 2$ |
| β⁻-decay | ${}^{A}_{Z}\mathrm{X} \rightarrow {}^{\ \ \ A}_{Z+1}\mathrm{Y} + {}^{\ \ 0}_{-1}\mathrm{e}$ | $A$ same, $Z + 1$; n → p + e |
| γ-emission | ${}^{A}_{Z}\mathrm{X}^{*} \rightarrow {}^{A}_{Z}\mathrm{X} + {}^{0}_{0}\gamma$ | no change |
| Fission | ${}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n} + {}^{235}_{\ 92}\mathrm{U} \rightarrow$ two nuclei $+ 2$–$3\,{}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}$ + energy | conserve $A$ and $Z$ |
| Fusion | ${}^{2}_{1}\mathrm{H} + {}^{3}_{1}\mathrm{H} \rightarrow {}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He} + {}^{1}_{0}\mathrm{n}$ + energy | conserve $A$ and $Z$ |
| Corrected count rate | measured − background | counts/s or counts/min |
| Fraction remaining | $\left(\tfrac12\right)^n$ after $n$ half-lives | |
| Orbital speed | $v = \dfrac{2\pi r}{T}$ | $r$ average orbital radius m, $T$ period s |
| Light travel time † | $t = \dfrac{d}{c}$ | s |
| Hubble's law | $H_0 = \dfrac{v}{d}$ | $H_0$ s⁻¹, $v$ m/s, $d$ m |
| Age of Universe | $\dfrac{d}{v} = \dfrac{1}{H_0}$ | s |

Light-year $\approx 9.5\times10^{15}$ m. $H_0 \approx 2.2\times10^{-18}$ s⁻¹. Milky Way diameter ≈ 100 000 light-years.
