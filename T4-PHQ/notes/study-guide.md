# Physics study guide (exam Tue 3 Nov)

**Exam:** Tuesday 3 November, 9 am. 3 hours. **No formula sheet**, so every equation comes from memory.

## The exam

| Paper | Time | What | Pace |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | 1 h | 40 multiple-choice questions, 1 mark each, answer grid | **1.5 min per question**. Paper 2 cannot start until the hour is up, so use spare time to recheck calculations. |
| Paper 2 | 2 h | 10 sections × 15 marks. Q1–8 theory, Q9–10 practical skills (data analysis, graph plotting, experiment design) | **12 min per section** (about 48 s per mark). If a section runs well past 12 min, move on and come back. |

Paper 1 tips: write the equation in the margin before choosing, even in multiple choice. Check that the answer has a sensible unit and power of ten (many wrong options are ×10³ or ÷2 slips). Answer every question. No penalty for wrong answers is stated.

## How every calculation must look

The 2024 scheme of work says that if there is no clear, correct physics equation, there are **no marks**, even with the right number. Examiners must see this format:

| Step | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Equation | the equation in symbols (or words), as you learnt it | $P = IV$ |
| 2. Substitution | the numbers in place of the symbols, **after** converting to SI units | $P = 0.25 \times 12$ |
| 3. Answer | the value, **unit**, and sensible significant figures | $P = 3.0$ W |

- Rearranging is fine, but write the original equation first, then the rearranged one.
- Convert units **before** substituting (km/h → m/s, g → kg, cm² → m², °C → K, minutes → s).
- Significant figures: the same as the least precise value given, usually 2 or 3. Never round in the middle. Keep the calculator value until the end.
- A missing or wrong unit normally loses the final mark. Moments are in N m, never J.
- In IGCSE mark schemes, a wrong final answer can still earn the equation and substitution marks, and a wrong earlier answer used correctly later still earns the later marks. Always show working.

Full details: [Units, symbols and rearranging](../notes/units-and-rearranging.md). Choosing the equation: [Which formula when](../notes/formula-chooser.md).

## Method for each kind of content

| Kind of content | Method |
|---|---|
| Equations | Anki cloze cards, then write the full formula sheet on a blank page and check it. |
| Choosing the equation | Trigger table in [Which formula when](../notes/formula-chooser.md) and its [deck](../anki/formula-chooser.txt). Before solving any question, list the givens with symbols and units. |
| Calculations | Cover the worked example's answer, solve it, compare line by line (equation → substitution → answer, unit, s.f.). Then do exam questions. |
| Explanations and causal chains | Write the chain from memory as arrows (e.g. fault → large current → fuse melts → live disconnected). Compare with the notes and add the missing link. |
| Definitions | Anki. Say the definition with its key words ("per unit", "perpendicular", "isolated"). |
| Graphs | Sketch each standard shape from memory. State what the gradient and area mean, with units. |
| Diagrams (rays, field lines, circuits) | Draw from a blank page, then check against the note's diagram. |
| Practical skills | Learn the checklists in [Practical skills](../notes/practical.md). Practise on 0625 Paper 6 questions and mark with the mark scheme. |

## Practice papers

No school past papers are held in this repo. Pre-Q is based on Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625, so use 0625 Extended papers:

| Pre-Q | Matching 0625 paper |
|---|---|
| Paper 1 (40 MCQ) | Paper 2, Multiple Choice (Extended), e.g. 0625/21, 22, 23 |
| Paper 2, Q1–8 (theory) | Paper 4, Theory (Extended), e.g. 0625/41, 42, 43 |
| Paper 2, Q9–10 (practical) | Paper 6, Alternative to Practical, e.g. 0625/61, 62, 63 |

0625 mark schemes use $g = 9.8$ m/s²; Pre-Q uses $g = 9.81$ m/s². Use whatever value a question gives. Skip 0625 questions on content Pre-Q does not cover (logic gates, beta-plus).
