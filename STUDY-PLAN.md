# Exam overview and priorities: Term 4 Pre-Q 2026

Six exams between Tuesday 27 October and Wednesday 4 November. This page doesn't give you a timetable. It says what matters most, in what order, and how to study each kind of content. Each subject's study guide ranks its own topics.

## Priority across subjects

1. **Maths, Economics, English.** These three come first and back to back (Maths and Economics on the same day), so they get the reading and practice before the exams start.
2. **Business, Geography, Physics.** Keep them alive with Anki now, and do their reading and practice in the gaps between exams. Spaced repetition works best on the exams furthest away.
3. **Economics and Geography are the hardest.** When something has to give, protect those two. Physics gets the least time (you're dropping it), but it still needs a solid pass through formulas and when to use them.

## How to study each kind of content

| Content | Method |
|---|---|
| Facts, definitions, quotes, formulas | Anki. Rate honestly: *Again* if you could not produce it. |
| Explanations, chains, models | Read one section → close the page → **blurt** (write or say everything you remember) → check and fix the gaps in a second colour. |
| Diagrams | Draw from memory with every label, then compare. |
| Maths and Physics methods | Past-paper questions → mark with the mark scheme → log every lost mark by type → redo the question a couple of days later. |
| Essays (English, Econ, Business, Geography) | Many quick plans for past questions; at least one full timed answer per question type. |
| The last day before an exam | Consolidate only: that subject's errors or traps page, the pre-exam Anki pass, sleep. Learn nothing new. |

## Anki

Every note is tagged `tier::1`, `tier::2` or `tier::3`. In every subject the tier 1 new cards come first.

**Deck options: one preset per subject** (Maths, Economics, English, Business, Geography, Physics), each saved to all its subdecks.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| FSRS | on | schedules each card by its predicted chance of being forgotten |
| Desired retention | 0.90 (English 0.92) | 0.95 would roughly double reviews; the pre-exam pass does the top-up |
| Learning steps | 1m 10m | |
| Relearning steps | 10m | |
| Maximum reviews/day | 9999 | a hidden backlog is worse than a long session |
| Leech action | Tag only | nothing gets suspended mid-cram |
| Bury new and review siblings | on | spreads cloze siblings across days |
| New card gather order | Ascending position | keeps tier 1 first |
| New card sort order | Random | |
| Review sort order | Retrievability ascending | the cards closest to being forgotten come first |
| New cards/day | Econ 70 · English 45 · Geo 40 · Maths 30 · Business 20 · Physics 15 | the biggest and soonest decks get the most |

**Adjusting new cards:**
- Two days before a subject's exam, set its new cards to 0. From then it gets reviews only.
- Once Maths, Economics and English are done, move that capacity to Business, Geography and Physics, roughly in that order.
- If reviews pile up, cut Geography's new cards first, then Physics'. Never cut the subject whose exam is next.

**FSRS Helper add-on** (`759844606`):
- Turn on *Auto disperse siblings* and *Auto reschedule cards reviewed on other devices after sync*.
- Under *Less Anki on Easy Days*, add your exam dates as specific dates.

**Pre-exam pass** (the evening before each exam):
- *Tools → Create Filtered Deck*, search `deck:"Economics" -is:new prop:r<0.92`, order *Retrievability ascending*, limit 300, *Reschedule cards* ticked.
- Delete the filtered deck afterwards; its cards return home.
- FSRS Helper → *Advance* does a similar job.

**After the exams:** suspend Physics. Keep the other five at 0.90, and after a few weeks of reviews run *Optimize* in each preset.
