Pre-Q Basic	Perpendicular height?	Height at right angles to the base, not a slant side	
Pre-Q Basic	Slant height (cone)?	Distance from apex to base edge along the surface	Not the same as h
Pre-Q Cloze	Area: rectangle {{c1::lw}}, triangle {{c2::\(\tfrac12 bh\)}}, parallelogram {{c3::bh}}	h = perpendicular height	
Pre-Q Cloze	Trapezium area \(= {{c1::\tfrac12(a+b)h}}\)	a and b are the parallel sides	
Pre-Q Cloze	Circle: area {{c1::\(\pi r^2\)}}; circumference {{c2::\(2\pi r\)}} or πd		
Pre-Q Cloze	Arc length \(= \frac{ {{c1::\theta}} }{360}\times {{c2::2\pi r}}\)	θ in degrees	Fraction of the circumference
Pre-Q Cloze	Sector area \(= \frac{\theta}{360}\times {{c1::\pi r^2}}\)		Fraction of the circle
Pre-Q Basic	Perimeter of a sector?	Arc length plus two radii	
Pre-Q Basic	Area of a segment?	Sector area − triangle area (\(\tfrac12 ab\sin C\))	
Pre-Q Basic	Area of a compound shape?	Split into standard shapes and add, or subtract a cut-out	
Pre-Q Basic	Perimeter of a shape with a corner cut out?	Not reduced; add every edge walked along	
Pre-Q Cloze	Cuboid: volume {{c1::lwh}}; surface area {{c2::\(2(lw+lh+wh)\)}}		
Pre-Q Cloze	Prism volume = {{c1::cross-section area}} × {{c2::length}}	Surface area = sum of all faces	
Pre-Q Cloze	Cylinder volume = {{c1::\(\pi r^2h\)}}	A prism with a circular cross-section	
Pre-Q Cloze	Closed cylinder surface area \(= {{c1::2\pi r^2}} + {{c2::2\pi rh}}\)	Open-topped: only one circle	Two ends + curved part
Pre-Q Basic	Why is a cylinder's curved surface \(2\pi rh\)?	It unrolls to a rectangle \(2\pi r\) wide and h high	
Pre-Q Cloze	Sphere: volume {{c1::\(\tfrac43\pi r^3\)}}; surface area {{c2::\(4\pi r^2\)}}	Given in the exam	
Pre-Q Cloze	Hemisphere: volume {{c1::\(\tfrac23\pi r^3\)}}; curved surface {{c2::\(2\pi r^2\)}}	Half a sphere	
Pre-Q Cloze	Cone: volume {{c1::\(\tfrac13\pi r^2h\)}}; curved surface {{c2::\(\pi rl\)}}	Given in the exam; add \(\pi r^2\) for the base	
Pre-Q Cloze	Cone slant height \(l = {{c1::\sqrt{r^2+h^2} }}\)		Pythagoras
Pre-Q Cloze	Pyramid volume = {{c1::\(\tfrac13\)}} × {{c2::base area}} × h	Given in the exam	
Pre-Q Cloze	Sector rolled into a cone: sector radius = cone's {{c1::slant height}}; arc = {{c2::base circumference}}	So \(\theta = \frac{360r}{l}\)	
Pre-Q Basic	Compound solid: is the joining face in the surface area?	No, it is internal	
Pre-Q Cloze	1 cm² = {{c1::100}} mm²; 1 m² = {{c2::10 000}} cm²		Square the length factor
Pre-Q Cloze	1 cm³ = {{c1::1000}} mm³; 1 m³ = {{c2::1 000 000}} cm³		Cube the length factor
Pre-Q Cloze	1 litre = {{c1::1000 cm³}}; 1 ml = {{c2::1 cm³}}; 1 m³ = {{c3::1000 litres}}		
Pre-Q Basic	Before substituting into a mensuration formula?	Convert all lengths to the same unit	
Pre-Q Basic	Value of π to use?	The calculator value; round only at the end
