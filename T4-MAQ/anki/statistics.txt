#deck:Maths::8 Statistics and probability
Probability formula	favourable outcomes / total outcomes
Range of the probability scale	0 (impossible) to 1 (certain)
Complement rule	\(P(\text{not }A) = 1 - P(A)\)
Relative frequency formula	number of successes / number of trials
What happens to relative frequency as trials increase?	It tends towards the theoretical probability
Expected frequency formula	probability × number of trials
What does a possibility diagram show?	All outcomes as points on a grid
Outcomes on a two-dice grid	36
P(total = 7) with two dice	6/36 = 1/6
Tree diagram: along the branches	Multiply (AND)
Tree diagram: between complete paths	Add (OR)
What must the branches from any one point sum to?	1
What changes in a without-replacement tree?	The second set of probabilities — the denominator drops by one
Bag of 5 red and 3 blue, two drawn without replacement — P(both red)?	\(\frac58\times\frac47 = \frac{5}{14}\)
How can you check a tree diagram?	All the complete-path probabilities should sum to 1
Difference between discrete and continuous data	Discrete takes separate values; continuous can take any value in a range
Pie chart angle formula	\(\frac{f}{\text{total}}\times360°\)
What is the key difference between a bar chart and a histogram?	A bar chart has gaps and equal widths; a histogram has no gaps and may have unequal widths
What does the AREA of a histogram bar represent?	The frequency
Frequency density formula	frequency / class width
What must the vertical axis of an unequal-interval histogram be labelled?	Frequency density
How do you get frequency back from a histogram?	Frequency density × class width
Mean formula	\(\frac{\sum x}{n}\)
Definition of the median	The middle value when the data is ordered
Median of an even number of values	The mean of the middle two
Definition of the mode	The most frequent value
Range formula	Highest − lowest
Which average is affected by outliers?	The mean — the median is not
When is the median the better average?	When there are extreme values
When is the mode the most useful?	For categorical data, or when you want the most common value
Mean from a frequency table	\(\frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}\)
Why is the mean of grouped data only an estimate?	You do not know the individual values, so you use class midpoints
Estimated mean of grouped data	\(\frac{\sum f\times\text{midpoint}}{\sum f}\)
What is the modal class?	The class with the highest frequency (highest frequency DENSITY if widths differ)
What do you plot cumulative frequency against?	The upper class boundary
Shape of a cumulative frequency curve	A smooth S-shape
Where do you read the median on a cumulative frequency curve?	At n/2
Where do you read the lower quartile?	At n/4
Where do you read the upper quartile?	At 3n/4
Interquartile range formula	\(Q_3 - Q_1\)
Why is the IQR better than the range?	It describes the middle half of the data and is unaffected by extreme values
What does a smaller IQR mean?	More consistent data
Positive correlation	As one variable increases, so does the other
Negative correlation	As one variable increases, the other decreases
What does strong correlation look like?	Points lying close to a straight line
How do you draw a line of best fit by eye?	Follow the trend with roughly equal numbers of points either side, through the mean point
Why is estimating outside the plotted range unreliable?	The trend is not known to continue beyond the data
Does correlation prove causation?	No
