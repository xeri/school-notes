#deck:Maths::6 Trigonometry
To what accuracy are angles required?	Degrees to one decimal place
Pythagoras' theorem	\(a^2+b^2 = c^2\), c being the hypotenuse
How do you find a shorter side with Pythagoras?	Subtract: \(a = \sqrt{c^2-b^2}\)
sin θ	opposite / hypotenuse
cos θ	adjacent / hypotenuse
tan θ	opposite / adjacent
Where is the hypotenuse?	Always opposite the right angle
What happens to "opposite" and "adjacent" if you switch acute angles?	They swap
How do you find an angle from a ratio?	Use the inverse function, e.g. \(\tan^{-1}\)
Angle of elevation	Measured UP from the horizontal
Angle of depression	Measured DOWN from the horizontal
Relationship between elevation and depression between two points	They are equal (alternate angles)
How are three-figure bearings measured?	Clockwise from North, written with three digits, 000°–360°
Bearing of East	090°
Bearing of South-west	225°
How do you write 7° east of north as a bearing?	007°
How do you find a back bearing?	Add 180° (or subtract 180° if over 360°)
Back bearing of 125°	305°
What should every bearings sketch include?	A North arrow at EACH point
\(\sin(180° - \theta)\)	\(\sin\theta\)
\(\cos(180° - \theta)\)	\(-\cos\theta\)
Value of sin 150°	0.5, same as sin 30°
Value of cos 150°	−0.866, the negative of cos 30°
Why can the sine rule give two answers?	\(\sin^{-1}\) returns only the acute angle; the obtuse option is 180° minus it
Sine rule	\(\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}\)
When do you use the sine rule?	When you have an angle and its opposite side, plus one more piece of information
Form of the sine rule for finding an angle	\(\frac{\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{b}\)
Cosine rule	\(a^2 = b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A\)
Cosine rule rearranged for an angle	\(\cos A = \frac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{2bc}\)
When do you use the cosine rule?	Two sides and the included angle, or all three sides
Why is the cosine rule unambiguous?	A negative cosine gives the obtuse angle directly
Area of a triangle (non-right-angled)	\(\tfrac12 ab\sin C\), C being the angle between a and b
Sides 5, 7, 9 — largest angle?	\(\cos A = (25+49-81)/70 = -0.1 \Rightarrow 95.7°\)
How do you tackle a 3-D trigonometry problem?	Draw one right-angled triangle at a time, separately from the 3-D figure
How do you find the angle between a line and a plane?	Drop a perpendicular to the plane and use the projection of the line on it
Cuboid 4 × 3 × 12 — base diagonal?	\(\sqrt{16+9} = 5\) cm
Cuboid 4 × 3 × 12 — space diagonal?	\(\sqrt{25+144} = 13\) cm
Cuboid 4 × 3 × 12 — angle of the space diagonal to the base?	\(\tan\theta = 12/5 \Rightarrow 67.4°\)
