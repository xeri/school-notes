#deck:Mathematics::1 Number
Is 1 a prime number?	No — it has only one factor
Which is the only even prime?	2
Definition of a rational number	One that can be written as a fraction a/b with integers a and b
Two examples of irrational numbers	π and √2
How do you find the HCF from prime factorisations?	Multiply the lowest power of each shared prime
How do you find the LCM from prime factorisations?	Multiply the highest power of every prime that appears
Relationship between HCF, LCM and the two numbers	HCF × LCM = the product of the two numbers
Meaning of \(x \in A\)	x is an element of A
Meaning of \(A'\)	The complement — everything not in A
Meaning of \(A \cup B\)	Union — in A or B (or both)
Meaning of \(A \cap B\)	Intersection — in A and B
Meaning of \(A \subset B\)	A is a proper subset of B
Symbol for the empty set	∅
Symbol for the universal set	Ԑ
What should you fill in first on a Venn diagram?	The intersection, then work outwards
Convert \(0.\dot7\) to a fraction	\(10x - x = 7 \Rightarrow x = 7/9\)
Convert \(0.\dot2\dot7\) to a fraction	\(100x - x = 27 \Rightarrow x = 27/99 = 3/11\)
Order of operations	Brackets, indices, division and multiplication, addition and subtraction
How do you divide by a fraction?	Multiply by its reciprocal
\(a^m \times a^n\)	\(a^{m+n}\)
\(a^m \div a^n\)	\(a^{m-n}\)
\((a^m)^n\)	\(a^{mn}\)
\(a^0\)	1
\(a^{-n}\)	\(1/a^n\)
\(a^{1/n}\)	The nth root of a
\(a^{m/n}\)	The nth root of a, all raised to the power m
Evaluate \(16^{3/4}\)	\((\sqrt[4]{16})^3 = 2^3 = 8\)
Evaluate \(25^{-1/2}\)	1/5
Range of A in standard form \(A\times10^n\)	\(1 \leqslant A < 10\)
Write 0.000082 in standard form	\(8.2\times10^{-5}\)
How do you count significant figures?	From the first non-zero digit
Bounds of a length given as 4.6 cm to 1 d.p.	\(4.55 \leqslant l < 4.65\)
How do you find the maximum of a product?	Multiply the two upper bounds
How do you find the maximum of a quotient a/b?	Largest numerator ÷ smallest denominator
Share £450 in the ratio 4:5:6	15 parts, so £120 : £150 : £180
Increase 80 in the ratio 7:5	\(80\times7/5 = 112\)
Equation for direct proportion	\(y = kx\)
Equation for inverse proportion	\(y = k/x\)
First step in any variation question	Find k from the given pair of values
Formula for average speed	Total distance ÷ total time (never the average of two speeds)
Multiplier to increase by 15%	×1.15
Multiplier to decrease by 15%	×0.85
How do you do a reverse percentage?	Divide by the multiplier, do not add the percentage back
A coat costs £84 after a 30% reduction — original price?	\(84 \div 0.70 = £120\)
Compound interest formula	\(P(1+r/100)^n\)
Depreciation formula	\(P(1-r/100)^n\)
Simple interest formula	\(I = Prt/100\)
£2000 at 4% compound for 5 years	\(2000\times1.04^5 = £2433.31\)
How do you write 14:35 as 12-hour time?	2:35 pm
