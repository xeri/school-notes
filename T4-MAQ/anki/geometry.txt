Angles at a point	Sum to 360°
Angles on a straight line	Sum to 180°
Vertically opposite angles	Equal
Corresponding angles (F shape)	Equal
Alternate angles (Z shape)	Equal
Co-interior angles (C shape)	Sum to 180°
Angles in a triangle	Sum to 180°
Exterior angle of a triangle	Equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles
Angles in a quadrilateral	Sum to 360°
Sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon	\((n-2)\times180°\)
Sum of exterior angles of any polygon	360°
Exterior angle of a regular n-sided polygon	\(360°/n\)
Interior angle of a regular octagon	\(180 - 45 = 135°\)
A regular polygon has interior angles of 162° — how many sides?	Exterior angle 18°, so \(360/18 = 20\) sides
Angle in a semicircle	90°
Angle between a tangent and a radius	90°
Angle at the centre vs angle at the circumference	The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference on the same arc
Angles in the same segment	Equal
Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral	Supplementary — sum to 180°
Equal chords in a circle	Are equidistant from the centre
Perpendicular bisector of a chord	Passes through the centre
Tangents from an external point	Are equal in length
What must you always include with an angle answer?	The name of the geometrical rule used as the reason
Properties of a rhombus	4 equal sides, opposite angles equal, diagonals bisect at 90°
Properties of a parallelogram	Opposite sides parallel and equal, opposite angles equal
Properties of a kite	Two pairs of adjacent equal sides, one line of symmetry
Definition of a trapezium	Exactly one pair of parallel sides
Difference between similar and congruent	Similar: same shape, sides in proportion. Congruent: identical in shape AND size
Lines of symmetry in a parallelogram	None
Order of rotational symmetry of a parallelogram	2
Order of rotational symmetry of a scalene triangle	1 (no rotational symmetry)
Planes of symmetry of a cylinder	Infinitely many
Area scale factor for similar figures	\(k^2\)
Volume scale factor for similar figures	\(k^3\)
Two similar cones, heights 6 cm and 9 cm — area scale factor?	\(k = 1.5\), so \(k^2 = 2.25\)
Volumes are 8 and 27 — length scale factor?	\(k = 3/2\)
How do you construct a triangle from three sides?	Draw the longest side, then intersecting arcs of the other two lengths from each end
How do you construct a perpendicular bisector?	Equal arcs above and below from each end, then join the intersections
Which points lie on a perpendicular bisector?	Those equidistant from the two end points
How do you construct an angle bisector?	Arc across both arms from the vertex, then equal arcs from each crossing, joined to the vertex
Which points lie on an angle bisector?	Those equidistant from the two arms
What must you NOT erase in a construction?	The construction arcs — they carry the marks
Scale 1:50 000 — what does 1 cm represent?	50 000 cm = 0.5 km
Locus of points a fixed distance from a point	A circle
Locus of points a fixed distance from a line	Two parallel lines either side (semicircular ends on a segment)
Locus of points equidistant from two points	The perpendicular bisector
Locus of points equidistant from two intersecting lines	The two angle bisectors
Solid or dashed boundary line for a strict inequality?	Dashed
