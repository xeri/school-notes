Expand \((x+4)(x-7)\)	\(x^2 - 3x - 28\)
Expand \((2x-3)^2\)	\(4x^2 - 12x + 9\)
Factorise \(9x^2+15xy\)	\(3x(3x+5y)\)
Factorise \(9x^2-25y^2\)	\((3x+5y)(3x-5y)\) — difference of two squares
Factorise \(x^2+10x+25\)	\((x+5)^2\) — perfect square
Factorise \(x^2+2x-15\)	\((x+5)(x-3)\)
Method for factorising \(ax+bx+kay+kby\)	Group in pairs and take out a common bracket
Method for factorising \(ax^2+bx+c\) when a ≠ 1	Find two numbers multiplying to ac and adding to b, split the middle term, then group
Factorise \(6x^2+11x-10\)	\((3x-2)(2x+5)\)
Simplify \(\frac{x^2+2x}{x^2+4x+4}\)	Factorise both: \(\frac{x(x+2)}{(x+2)^2} = \frac{x}{x+2}\)
Can you cancel individual terms in a fraction?	No — only common factors, after factorising
Simplify \(\frac{2x}{3}\div\frac{4x}{9}\)	\(\frac{2x}{3}\times\frac{9}{4x} = \frac32\)
Simplify \(\left(\frac{2x^5}{3}\right)^3\)	\(\frac{8x^{15}}{27}\)
Solve \(32^x = 2\)	\((2^5)^x = 2^1 \Rightarrow x = 1/5\)
How do you rearrange a formula when the subject appears twice?	Gather its terms on one side and factorise it out
What is the one special rule for inequalities?	Multiplying or dividing by a negative number reverses the inequality sign
Solve \(-3x+2 \leqslant 11\)	\(-3x \leqslant 9 \Rightarrow x \geqslant -3\)
Number-line notation for \(\leqslant\)	A filled circle (open circle for < and >)
Elimination method for simultaneous equations	Scale the equations to match one coefficient, then add or subtract
How do you check a simultaneous equation answer?	Substitute into the equation you did not use for the second unknown
Graphical meaning of simultaneous equations	The point where the two lines cross
Quadratic formula	\(x = \frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\)
Completing the square for \(x^2+bx+c\)	\(\left(x+\frac b2\right)^2 - \frac{b^2}{4} + c\)
Complete the square: \(x^2+6x-1\)	\((x+3)^2 - 10\)
Turning point of \(y = (x+3)^2-10\)	\((-3,-10)\)
What do you do first if a ≠ 1 when completing the square?	Factor a out of the whole expression
What is the discriminant?	\(b^2-4ac\)
Discriminant positive, zero, negative — how many roots?	Two, one repeated, none
Which quadratic method also gives the turning point?	Completing the square
nth term of a linear sequence	\(dn + (a_1 - d)\), where d is the first difference
nth term of 5, 9, 13, 17	\(4n+1\)
How do you spot a quadratic sequence?	The second differences are constant
Coefficient of \(n^2\) in a quadratic sequence	Half the second difference
nth term of 3, 8, 15, 24	\(n^2+2n\)
Coefficient of \(n^3\) in a cubic sequence	The third difference ÷ 6
nth term of a geometric sequence	\(ar^{n-1}\)
nth term of 3, 6, 12, 24	\(3\times2^{n-1}\)
Write "y is inversely proportional to the square of x"	\(y = k/x^2\)
Meaning of \(f(x) = 3x-5\)	A function that triples the input and subtracts 5
How do you find an inverse function?	Write y = f(x), rearrange to make x the subject, then swap the letters
Inverse of \(f(x) = 3x-5\)	\(f^{-1}(x) = \frac{x+5}{3}\)
Meaning of \(gf(x)\)	Apply f first, then g — g[f(x)]
With \(f(x)=3x-5\) and \(g(x)=x^2\), what is gf(x)?	\((3x-5)^2\)
With \(f(x)=3x-5\) and \(g(x)=x^2\), what is fg(x)?	\(3x^2-5\)
Does \(gf(x) = fg(x)\)?	Not in general — the order matters
How do you find where a line meets a curve?	Substitute the line into the curve, solve the quadratic, then find each y
Where does \(y = x+1\) meet \(y = x^2-5\)?	\(x^2-x-6 = 0\), so (3, 4) and (−2, −1)
Solve \(x^2 \leqslant 9\)	\(-3 \leqslant x \leqslant 3\) (between the roots)
Solve \(x^2-x-6 > 0\)	\(x < -2\) or \(x > 3\) (outside the roots)
